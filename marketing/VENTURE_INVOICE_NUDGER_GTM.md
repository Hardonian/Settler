# Settler Venture Invoice Nudger — GTM Brief

## Positioning

**Promise:** Recover receivables faster without embarrassing false reminders.

Settler’s Venture Invoice Nudger combines overdue invoice detection with reconciliation signals so teams send fewer wrong nudges and get paid sooner.

## ICP (Initial)

1. B2B SaaS (seed to Series B) with manual AR follow-up
2. Agencies/consultancies with recurring net-30 invoicing
3. Lean finance ops teams running Stripe + accounting connectors

## Core Pain Points

- Late invoices are found too late
- Teams send reminders after payment is already in-flight
- No audit trail explaining why a reminder was sent/suppressed

## Product Narrative

1. Settler ingests invoice + transaction + reconciliation context
2. Nudger scores each overdue invoice with payment-signal suppression
3. Team reviews/approves or auto-runs policy
4. Settler tracks outcome and continuously improves thresholds

## Message Pillars

- **Accuracy:** Fewer false reminders through recon-aware suppression
- **Speed:** Time-to-first-followup reduced from days to minutes
- **Control:** Full run-level and invoice-level decision audit trail

## Launch Assets (Phase 1)

- One-page product explainer
- Demo script: dry-run to execute workflow
- 3 outbound email templates (CFO, Controller, Founder/COO)
- ROI calculator: DSO reduction + recovered cash projection

## Pricing Sketch

- Base platform fee + usage-based nudger volume
- Premium: advanced policy engine + auto-execute + channel integrations
- Enterprise: custom risk controls, SLA, approval routing

## KPIs

- Recovery rate uplift (%)
- Median days-to-payment reduction
- False nudge rate (should trend down)
- Operator time saved per month

## 30-Day Plan

- Week 1: pilot design partners + dry-run baseline
- Week 2: approve templates + decision policy tuning
- Week 3: execute mode with human-in-loop approvals
- Week 4: publish pilot metrics + case-study snippets
