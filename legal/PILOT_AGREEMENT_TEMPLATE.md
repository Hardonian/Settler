# Pilot Agreement Template

**Last Updated:** 2025-01-20  
**Status:** Revenue Bootstrap - Active  
**Purpose:** Lightweight pilot agreement template for customer pilots

## Overview

This is a **lightweight, modular pilot agreement** designed for:
- Low-friction pilot signups
- Clear scope and responsibilities
- Easy conversion to paid contracts

**Philosophy:** Keep it simple, avoid overpromising, make it easy to say yes.

---

## Pilot Agreement

**Parties:**
- **Provider:** Settler, Inc. ("Settler")
- **Customer:** [Customer Name] ("Customer")

**Effective Date:** [Date]

**Pilot Period:** 14 days from Effective Date

---

## 1. Pilot Scope

### 1.1 Included Services
During the Pilot Period, Settler will provide:
- Access to Settler's reconciliation, receipts, and feature flags APIs
- Developer Console access
- Standard integrations (Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks, PayPal, Xero)
- Email support (24-48 hour response)
- Documentation and guides

### 1.2 Excluded Services
The following are **explicitly excluded**:
- Custom integrations
- On-premise deployment
- White-label solutions
- Dedicated support
- SLA guarantees
- Custom contract terms

---

## 2. Pilot Period

### 2.1 Duration
The Pilot Period begins on the Effective Date and continues for 14 days.

### 2.2 Extension
The Pilot Period may be extended by mutual agreement, not to exceed 30 days total.

### 2.3 Termination
Either party may terminate the Pilot Period at any time with written notice.

---

## 3. Customer Responsibilities

### 3.1 Data
Customer will provide:
- API access to Customer's systems (Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks, etc.)
- Sample data for testing (at least 1K transactions)
- Contact information and use case details

### 3.2 Testing
Customer will:
- Complete onboarding within 7 days
- Run at least one successful reconciliation
- Provide feedback on pilot experience

### 3.3 Compliance
Customer will:
- Comply with Settler's Terms of Service
- Not use Pilot for production workloads without paid plan
- Not share Pilot access with unauthorized users

---

## 4. Settler Responsibilities

### 4.1 Services
Settler will:
- Provide access to Settler platform during Pilot Period
- Respond to support requests within 24-48 hours
- Provide documentation and guides

### 4.2 Support
Settler will provide:
- Email support (support@settler.io)
- Onboarding guidance
- Documentation access

---

## 5. Pilot Conversion

### 5.1 Conversion Criteria
Pilot converts to paid plan if Customer:
- Completes onboarding within 7 days
- Runs at least one successful reconciliation
- Processes at least 1K transactions
- Achieves 95%+ match rate
- Logs in at least 3 times

### 5.2 Conversion Process
Upon meeting conversion criteria:
- Customer may upgrade to paid plan
- Pilot Period ends, paid plan begins
- Standard billing terms apply

### 5.3 No Conversion
If Pilot does not convert:
- Pilot Period ends
- Access revoked after 7-day grace period
- No obligation to continue

---

## 6. Data & Privacy

### 6.1 Data Ownership
Customer retains ownership of all Customer data.

### 6.2 Data Usage
Settler will:
- Use Customer data only to provide services
- Not share Customer data with third parties
- Delete Customer data upon Pilot termination (unless converted)

### 6.3 Privacy
Settler's Privacy Policy applies: https://settler.dev/privacy

---

## 7. Limitation of Liability

### 7.1 No Warranty
Settler provides Pilot services "as-is" without warranty.

### 7.2 Limitation
Settler's liability is limited to the amount paid by Customer (zero for Pilot).

### 7.3 No SLA
No SLA guarantees during Pilot Period.

---

## 8. Termination

### 8.1 Termination by Customer
Customer may terminate Pilot at any time with written notice.

### 8.2 Termination by Settler
Settler may terminate Pilot if Customer:
- Violates Terms of Service
- Abuses Pilot access
- Uses Pilot for production workloads without paid plan

### 8.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination:
- Access revoked after 7-day grace period
- Customer data deleted after 30 days
- No refunds (Pilot is free)

---

## 9. General Terms

### 9.1 Entire Agreement
This Pilot Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.

### 9.2 Modification
This Pilot Agreement may be modified only by mutual written agreement.

### 9.3 Governing Law
This Pilot Agreement is governed by [State] law.
