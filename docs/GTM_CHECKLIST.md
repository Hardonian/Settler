# Go-To-Market Execution Checklist

**Last Updated:** 2025-01-20  
**Status:** Revenue Bootstrap - Active  
**Purpose:** Turn strategy into motion - executable by one person

## Overview

This checklist helps you execute the GTM strategy:
- **Outreach sequences:** Who to contact, when, how
- **Pilot offer language:** What to say, how to present
- **Demo flow:** How to demo, what to show
- **Follow-up cadence:** When to follow up, what to say
- **Close criteria:** When to close, how to close

**Philosophy:** Make this runnable by one person (solo founder).

**"I'd like to offer you a 14-day pilot of Settler. Here's what's included:**

- **Full API Access:** All reconciliation, receipts, and feature flags APIs
- **Unlimited Usage:** No usage limits during pilot
- **Standard Integrations:** Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks, PayPal, Xero
- **Developer Console:** Full access to Console dashboard
- **Email Support:** 24-48 hour response
- **No Credit Card Required:** Free to try

**What you'll need:**
- API access to your systems (Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks)
- Sample data (at least 1K transactions)
- 30 minutes to set up

**After 14 days:**
- If you see value, upgrade to a paid plan ($99/month Starter)
- If not, no obligation—pilot expires

**Ready to start?** Sign up at [settler.dev/signup] or reply to this email."

## Demo Flow

### Demo Structure (30 minutes)
1. **Opening (5 min):** Problem discovery, use case confirmation
2. **Demo (15 min):** Show Console, run reconciliation, show results
3. **Value (5 min):** Show time savings, accuracy improvement
4. **Close (5 min):** Pilot offer, next steps

### Demo Script
**Opening:**
"Thanks for your time. Before I show you Settler, I'd like to understand your situation. What's your current reconciliation process? How long does it take?"

**Demo:**
"Let me show you Settler. This is the Developer Console. Here's how you connect your APIs—Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks. Here's how you create a reconciliation job. Here's how you run it. Here are the results—matched transactions, unmatched items, complete audit trail."

**Value:**
"This reconciliation processed 10K transactions in 5 minutes. 99.5% matched automatically. This would have taken 20 hours manually. With Settler, it took 5 minutes."

**Close:**
"Would you like to try a 14-day pilot? No credit card required. You can set it up in 30 minutes and see value immediately."

- **Day 1:** Welcome email, onboarding guide
- **Day 7:** Check-in: "How's it going? Any questions?"

## Close Criteria

### Ready to Close (Convert to Paid)
**Customer should convert if:**
- ✅ Meets all primary success criteria (see `/docs/PILOT_SUCCESS_CRITERIA.md`)
- ✅ Sees clear ROI (time saved, accuracy improved)
- ✅ Has budget approved
- ✅ Plans to use Settler ongoing

**Close Language:**
"Based on your pilot results, Settler looks like a great fit. You've processed [X] transactions, achieved [Y]% accuracy, and saved [Z] hours. Ready to upgrade to the Starter plan ($99/month)?"

---

### Not Ready to Close (Extend or Stop)
**Customer should extend if:**
- ⚠️ Needs more time to evaluate
- ⚠️ Budget approval pending
- ⚠️ Needs to test specific use case

**Extend Language:**
"I understand you need more time to evaluate. Would you like to extend your pilot for another 14 days?"

**Customer should stop if:**
- ❌ Doesn't meet success criteria
- ❌ Doesn't see ROI
- ❌ Doesn't have budget
- ❌ Wrong use case

**Stop Language:**
"I understand Settler may not be the right fit right now. I'll reconnect in 3 months to see if your situation has changed."
