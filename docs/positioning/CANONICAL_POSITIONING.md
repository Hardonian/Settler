# Canonical Positioning

## One-line summary

Settler is an open-source reconciliation engine that finds mismatches across systems and produces replayable, evidence-backed runs.

## What Settler does today

- Runs reconciliation workflows.
- Surfaces mismatches with contextual details.
- Exports evidence artifacts for audit and incident review.
- Replays prior runs against captured evidence inputs.

## What we avoid claiming

- We do not claim universal correctness for all customer data without validation.
- We do not claim zero-manual-review workflows.
- We do not claim enterprise-only capabilities are part of OSS unless implemented in this repo.

## Messaging guardrails

- Use plain language before technical detail.
- Prefer “replayable evidence-backed runs” over vague trust language.
- Distinguish “available now” from “roadmap”.
- When uncertain, narrow claims to verifiable behavior in docs or tests.
