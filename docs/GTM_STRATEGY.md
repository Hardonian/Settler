**Date:** 2025-01-27  
**Status:** Launch-Ready

---

## OVERVIEW

**ICP:** B2B SaaS operators (5-200 employees) doing month-end reconciliation.

**Value Proposition:** Stop spending 8-16 hours per month manually reconciling transactions. Automate 95%+ of the work. Save $300-700/month in labor costs. Reduce audit risk.

**Positioning:** Reconciliation as a system, not a human task. Built for operators, not accountants.

## SALES MOTION

### Conversation-First Outbound

**Principle:** Non-salesy discovery. Help first, sell second.

**Target:** B2B SaaS operators (5-200 employees) doing month-end reconciliation.

**Outreach Channels:**

- LinkedIn (founder-led, personal messages)
- Email (warm introductions, cold outreach)
- Community engagement (Indie Hackers, SaaS communities)

**Discovery Questions:**

- "How do you currently handle month-end reconciliation?"
- "How long does it take you to reconcile Stripe payments with Shopify orders?"
- "What's your biggest pain point with reconciliation?"
- "Have you looked into automation solutions?"

**Qualification Signals:**

- ✅ Spending 8+ hours/month on reconciliation
- ✅ Manual process (spreadsheets, custom scripts)
- ✅ Multiple platforms (Stripe + Shopify, QuickBooks, etc.)
- ✅ Compliance requirements (audit trail, month-end reports)
- ❌ Building a fintech app (not our ICP)
- ❌ Enterprise (>200 employees, need custom integrations)
- ❌ Consumer (not our ICP)

**Sales Narrative:**

**Opening:**
"I noticed you're using Stripe and Shopify. How do you handle month-end reconciliation between the two?"

**If they say "manually" or "spreadsheets":**
"That's exactly what we built Settler to solve. Most B2B SaaS operators spend 8-16 hours per month manually matching transactions. Settler automates 95%+ of that work. Want to see how it works?"

**If they say "we have a custom script":**
"Custom scripts work until they break. Settler is built specifically for reconciliation—deterministic matching, complete audit trail, 50+ platform integrations. Want to see how it compares?"

**If they say "we use [competitor]":**
"That's great. What's your biggest pain point with [competitor]? We built Settler specifically for B2B SaaS operators doing month-end reconciliation. Want to see how we compare?"

**Closing:**
"Want to try it? 14-day free trial, no credit card required. You can run your first reconciliation in 15 minutes."

**Who This Is NOT For:**

- Fintech builders (we're not a fintech infrastructure platform)
- Enterprises (>200 employees, need custom integrations—we have Enterprise plans but focus on 5-200)
- Consumers (we're B2B only)
- Real-time matching (we're month-end reconciliation, not real-time)

### Acquisition Metrics:
