## 💡 Pro Tips

### Time Management
- **Batch similar tasks** (check all dashboards at once)
- **Use templates** (emails, social posts)
- **Automate what you can** (alerts, notifications)
- **Focus on high-impact** (customer acquisition > perfecting code)

### Prioritization
- **Critical:** Blocks revenue → Do now
- **High:** Improves revenue → Do this month
- **Medium:** Improves retention → Do next month
- **Low:** Nice to have → Do when revenue allows

### Common Pitfalls
- ❌ **Perfectionism:** Ship fast, iterate
- ❌ **Feature Creep:** Focus on core reconciliation
- ❌ **Ignoring Customers:** Respond to every customer
- ❌ **Burnout:** Set boundaries, take breaks
