# Expansion Revenue Strategy

## Overview

Maximize revenue from existing customers through upsells, add-ons, and usage-based expansion.

## Expansion Levers

### 1. Plan Upgrades

- **Free → Commercial:** $0 → $99/month
- **Commercial → Enterprise:** $99 → $500/month
- **Trigger:** Usage approaching limits, feature needs

### 2. Add-Ons

- **Additional Integrations:** $50/month per integration beyond 5
- **Extended Data Retention:** $10/month per additional month
- **Priority Support:** $100/month
- **Custom Matching Rules:** $200/month

### 3. Usage-Based Expansion

- **Transaction Overage:** $0.10 per 1,000 transactions
- **API Calls:** $0.01 per 1,000 calls
- **Storage:** $0.05 per GB/month

### 4. Enterprise Features

- **Dedicated Support:** $500/month
- **SLA Guarantees:** $300/month
- **Custom Integrations:** $1,000+ one-time
- **On-Premise Deployment:** Custom pricing

## Expansion Triggers

### Automated

- Usage approaching limits (80% threshold)
- High churn risk detected
- Expansion opportunity score > 0.6
- Trial ending soon

### Manual

- Quarterly business reviews
- Feature requests
- Support interactions
- Contract renewals

## Expansion Campaigns

### Email Sequences

- Usage-based upgrade prompts
- Feature unlock emails
- Success story sharing
- ROI calculators

### In-App

- Upgrade banners
- Feature comparison modals
- Usage dashboards
- Upgrade CTAs

### Sales Outreach

- Enterprise account management
- Custom solution proposals
- ROI analysis
- Reference customers

## Metrics

### Expansion Revenue

- **Target:** 20% of MRR from expansion
- **Current:** Track monthly
- **Goal:** $50K MRR from expansion by end of year

### Expansion Rate

- **Target:** 15% of customers expand annually
- **Measurement:** % of customers who upgrade/add-ons
- **Goal:** Increase to 25% by Q4

### Net Revenue Retention

- **Target:** 120%+
- **Calculation:** (Starting MRR + Expansion - Churn) / Starting MRR
- **Goal:** Maintain 120%+ consistently

## Tactics

### 1. Value-Based Upselling

- Show ROI of upgrade
- Highlight time savings
- Demonstrate cost efficiency
- Provide case studies

### 2. Feature Gating

- Soft paywalls for premium features
- Trial periods for enterprise features
- Usage-based feature unlocks

### 3. Success-Based Expansion

- Celebrate milestones
- Suggest next steps
- Offer expansion incentives
- Provide upgrade paths
