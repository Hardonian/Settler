# Discovery Questions

**Last Updated:** 2025-01-20  
**Status:** Revenue Bootstrap - Active  
**Purpose:** Discovery questions to understand customer needs and qualify prospects

## Overview

Discovery questions help sales teams:

- **Understand needs:** What problem are they solving?
- **Qualify prospects:** Do they fit our ICP?
- **Uncover pain:** What's the real pain point?
- **Build rapport:** Show you understand their situation

**Philosophy:** Ask open-ended questions, listen more than you talk.

---

## Problem Discovery

### Current State

1. **"What's your current reconciliation process?"**
   - Understand: Manual vs automated, tools used, workflow
   - Listen for: Time-consuming, error-prone, doesn't scale

2. **"How long does reconciliation take?"**
   - Understand: Time spent per month, frequency
   - Listen for: 2+ days/month, weekly/monthly process

3. **"What's your error rate?"**
   - Understand: Accuracy, mismatch rate, discrepancies
   - Listen for: 5%+ error rate, frequent discrepancies

4. **"What systems do you need to reconcile between?"**
   - Understand: Payment processors, e-commerce, accounting
   - Listen for: Stripe + Shopify + QuickBooks, multiple systems

---

## Use Case Discovery

### Use Case

1. **"What's your use case? (Stripe + Shopify + QuickBooks?)"**
   - Understand: Specific use case, systems involved
   - Listen for: Multi-system reconciliation, standard integrations

2. **"How many transactions do you process per month?"**
   - Understand: Volume, scale
   - Listen for: 10K+ transactions/month, growing volume

3. **"What's your current volume?"**
   - Understand: Current vs expected volume
   - Listen for: Growing, scaling, volume increases

4. **"Do you have multi-currency transactions?"**
   - Understand: Currency complexity
   - Listen for: Multiple currencies, currency conversion issues

---

## Authority Discovery

### Decision Making

1. **"Who makes budget decisions for tools like this?"**
   - Understand: Decision maker, approval process
   - Listen for: Finance Manager, CFO, Operations Lead

2. **"What's your budget approval process?"**
   - Understand: Approval workflow, timeline
   - Listen for: Can approve $100-$500/month, needs approval

3. **"Who else needs to be involved in the decision?"**
   - Understand: Stakeholders, decision committee
   - Listen for: IT, Finance, Operations, single decision maker

4. **"What's your timeline for making a decision?"**
   - Understand: Urgency, decision timeline
   - Listen for: Within 30 days, urgent, specific date

## Budget Discovery

### Budget & ROI

1. **"What's your budget for reconciliation tools?"**
   - Understand: Budget range, approval status
   - Listen for: $100-$500/month, budget approved or pending

2. **"How do you justify tool purchases? (ROI, cost savings?)"**
   - Understand: Purchase criteria, ROI expectations
   - Listen for: Time savings, cost savings, efficiency

3. **"What's your current cost? (labor, errors, time?)"**
   - Understand: Current cost, pain cost
   - Listen for: $2K-$5K/month in labor, error costs

4. **"What would make this a no-brainer purchase?"**
   - Understand: Decision criteria, must-haves
   - Listen for: Time savings, accuracy, scalability

## Pain Discovery

### Pain Points

1. **"What's the biggest pain point with your current process?"**
   - Understand: Primary pain, frustration
   - Listen for: Time-consuming, error-prone, doesn't scale

2. **"What happens when reconciliation is wrong?"**
   - Understand: Impact of errors, consequences
   - Listen for: Compliance risk, audit issues, customer impact

3. **"How does this affect your team?"**
   - Understand: Team impact, morale
   - Listen for: Overwhelmed, frustrated, can't scale

4. **"What would success look like?"**
   - Understand: Desired outcome, success criteria
   - Listen for: Time savings, accuracy, scalability

## Technical Discovery

### Technical Requirements

1. **"Do you have API access to your systems?"**
   - Understand: API availability, technical capability
   - Listen for: Yes, standard APIs, can integrate

2. **"What's your technical setup?"**
   - Understand: Infrastructure, technical stack
   - Listen for: Cloud-based, API-friendly, modern stack

3. **"Do you have a technical team?"**
   - Understand: Technical resources, integration capability
   - Listen for: Yes, can integrate, technical capability

4. **"What integrations do you currently use?"**
   - Understand: Integration experience, existing integrations
   - Listen for: Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks, API integrations

## Timeline Discovery

### Timeline & Urgency

1. **"When do you need this solved?"**
   - Understand: Urgency, timeline
   - Listen for: Within 30 days, urgent, specific date

2. **"What's driving the timeline?"**
   - Understand: Trigger event, catalyst
   - Listen for: Scaling, errors, compliance, audit

3. **"What happens if you don't solve this?"**
   - Understand: Consequences, cost of inaction
   - Listen for: Can't scale, compliance risk, team overwhelmed

4. **"What's your ideal timeline?"**
   - Understand: Desired timeline, expectations
   - Listen for: ASAP, within 30 days, specific date

## Discovery Best Practices

### Do's

- ✅ **Ask open-ended questions:** "What's your current process?" (not "Do you use Excel?")
- ✅ **Listen more than you talk:** 70% listening, 30% talking
- ✅ **Follow up:** "Tell me more about that" (dig deeper)
- ✅ **Take notes:** Document answers, track qualification

### Don'ts

- ❌ **Don't pitch:** Discovery is about understanding, not selling
- ❌ **Don't interrupt:** Let them finish their thoughts
- ❌ **Don't assume:** Ask, don't assume you know their situation
- ❌ **Don't rush:** Take time to understand their needs

## Discovery Script Template

### Opening

"Thanks for your interest in Settler. I'd like to understand your situation better so I can see if we're a good fit. Is that okay?"

### Problem Discovery

"Let's start with your current process. What's your current reconciliation process? How long does it take? What's your error rate?"

### Use Case Discovery

"What systems do you need to reconcile between? How many transactions do you process per month?"

### Authority Discovery

"Who makes budget decisions for tools like this? What's your budget approval process?"

### Budget Discovery

"What's your budget for reconciliation tools? How do you justify tool purchases?"

### Closing

"Based on what you've told me, Settler sounds like a good fit. Would you like to try a 14-day pilot?"
