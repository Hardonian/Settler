# Who This Is NOT For

**Last Updated:** 2025-01-20  
**Status:** Revenue Bootstrap - Active  
**Purpose:** Explicitly exclude bad-fit customers to avoid wasted sales cycles

## Overview

Settler is **not** for everyone. This document explicitly defines who we **don't** sell to, why, and how to politely disqualify them early.

**Philosophy:** It's better to say "no" early than waste time on bad-fit customers who will churn or never convert.

---

## Exclusion #1: Companies Without Reconciliation Pain

### Who
- Companies doing <1K transactions/month
- Companies with simple, single-channel operations
- Companies where reconciliation is already automated
- Companies where reconciliation takes <1 hour/month

### Why Not
- **No real pain:** They don't have enough volume to justify automation
- **Low value:** They won't see ROI
- **High churn risk:** They'll cancel after trial
- **Support burden:** They'll need hand-holding for low-value accounts

### How to Disqualify
- **Discovery question:** "How many transactions do you reconcile per month?"
- **Disqualify if:** <1K transactions/month
- **Response:** "Settler is designed for companies doing 10K+ transactions/month. For smaller volumes, manual reconciliation or spreadsheets are usually sufficient. I'd be happy to reconnect when you're processing more volume."

---

## Exclusion #2: Companies Without Multi-System Integration

### Who
- Companies using only one payment processor
- Companies with no accounting system integration
- Companies doing manual data entry only
- Companies without API access to their systems

### Why Not
- **No integration need:** They don't have multiple systems to reconcile
- **Low value:** Settler's core value is multi-system reconciliation
- **Wrong use case:** They need different tools (accounting software, payment processor features)
- **Support burden:** They'll struggle with integration setup

### How to Disqualify
- **Discovery question:** "What systems do you need to reconcile between?"
- **Disqualify if:** Only one system, or no API access
- **Response:** "Settler is designed for companies reconciling between multiple systems (e.g., Stripe + Shopify + QuickBooks). If you're only using one system, you might be better served by that system's native features. I'd be happy to reconnect when you're integrating multiple systems."

---

## Exclusion #3: Companies Without Budget Authority

### Who
- Individual contributors without budget approval
- Students or hobbyists
- Companies without SaaS tool budget
- Companies requiring >$1K/month approval but can't get it

### Why Not
- **No buying power:** They can't approve purchases
- **Long sales cycle:** They'll need to get approval (may never happen)
- **Low conversion:** They'll churn or never convert
- **Time waste:** Wasted sales cycles

### How to Disqualify
- **Discovery question:** "What's your budget approval process for tools like this?"
- **Disqualify if:** No budget authority, or >$1K/month approval required
- **Response:** "Settler requires budget approval. I'd be happy to connect with your finance/operations team who has budget authority. Can you introduce me to the decision maker?"

---

## Exclusion #4: Companies Requiring Custom Development

### Who
- Companies needing extensive custom features
- Companies requiring on-premise deployment
- Companies needing white-label solutions
- Companies requiring custom integrations we don't support

### Why Not
- **Not our product:** We're a SaaS platform, not a custom development shop
- **High cost:** Custom development is expensive and not scalable
- **Long timeline:** Custom work takes months, not weeks
- **Wrong fit:** They need a different solution (custom development, enterprise platform)

### How to Disqualify
- **Discovery question:** "What specific features or integrations do you need?"
- **Disqualify if:** Extensive custom requirements, on-premise, or white-label
- **Response:** "Settler is a SaaS platform with standard integrations. If you need extensive custom development or on-premise deployment, we're not the right fit. I'd be happy to recommend custom development partners or enterprise platforms that might be better suited."

---

## Exclusion #5: Companies in Highly Regulated Industries (Without Enterprise Plan)

### Who
- Healthcare companies (HIPAA requirements)
- Financial services (banking, insurance) requiring extensive compliance
- Government contractors requiring FedRAMP
- Companies requiring industry-specific certifications we don't have

### Why Not
- **Compliance gaps:** We don't have all required certifications
- **High risk:** Compliance failures create legal risk
- **Wrong fit:** They need enterprise-grade compliance we may not offer
- **Long sales cycle:** Compliance reviews take months

### How to Disqualify
- **Discovery question:** "What compliance or regulatory requirements do you have?"
- **Disqualify if:** HIPAA, FedRAMP, or industry-specific certifications we don't have
- **Response:** "Settler is designed for e-commerce and SaaS companies. If you have HIPAA, FedRAMP, or other industry-specific compliance requirements, we may not be the right fit. I'd be happy to discuss our Enterprise plan if you're interested, but we may not meet all your compliance needs."

---

## Exclusion #6: Companies Looking for Free/Open Source

### Who
- Companies wanting free/open source solutions
- Companies unwilling to pay for SaaS tools
- Companies expecting free support or custom development
- Companies with no budget for tools

### Why Not
- **No revenue:** They won't pay
- **Wrong expectations:** They expect free support and features
- **High churn risk:** They'll cancel when asked to pay
- **Time waste:** Wasted sales cycles

### How to Disqualify
- **Discovery question:** "What's your budget for reconciliation tools?"
- **Disqualify if:** $0 budget, or expecting free/open source
- **Response:** "Settler is a paid SaaS platform starting at $99/month. If you're looking for a free or open source solution, we're not the right fit. I'd be happy to recommend open source alternatives if you're interested."

---

## Exclusion #7: Companies Without Technical Capability

### Who
- Companies without API access to their systems
- Companies without technical team to integrate
- Companies requiring extensive hand-holding for basic setup
- Companies expecting us to build integrations for them

### Why Not
- **Integration complexity:** They can't integrate without help
- **High support burden:** They'll need extensive hand-holding
- **Low success rate:** They'll struggle to get value
- **Wrong fit:** They need managed services, not self-service SaaS

### How to Disqualify
- **Discovery question:** "Do you have API access to your systems, and a technical team to integrate?"
- **Disqualify if:** No API access, or no technical capability
- **Response:** "Settler requires API access to your systems and basic technical capability to integrate. If you don't have API access or a technical team, we may not be the right fit. I'd be happy to recommend managed services or integration partners that might be better suited."

## Summary

### Quick Disqualification Checklist

**Disqualify if:**
- ✅ <1K transactions/month
- ✅ Only one system (no multi-system reconciliation)
- ✅ No budget authority or $0 budget
- ✅ Extensive custom requirements or on-premise needs
- ✅ HIPAA/FedRAMP/industry-specific compliance we don't have
- ✅ Expecting free/open source solution
- ✅ No API access or technical capability

**Qualify if:**
- ✅ 10K+ transactions/month
- ✅ Multiple systems to reconcile (Stripe + Shopify + QuickBooks, etc.)
- ✅ Budget authority for $100-$500/month
- ✅ Standard integrations sufficient
- ✅ E-commerce/SaaS industry (not highly regulated)
- ✅ Willing to pay for SaaS tools
- ✅ API access and basic technical capability
