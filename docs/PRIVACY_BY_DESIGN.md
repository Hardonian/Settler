# Privacy Control Boundary

Privacy-oriented architecture and data-subject workflow code do not establish legal compliance.
Deployment-specific data flows, roles, subprocessors, retention, residency, deletion behavior, and
contract terms require review by qualified counsel and the customer.

Do not use this document as a GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, or other legal conclusion. The superseded
self-assessment is archived under `docs/archive/2026-09-21-evidence-audit/`.
