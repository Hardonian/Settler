## Partnership Types

### 1. Integration Partnerships

- **Goal:** Native integrations
- **Benefit:** Easier setup, better UX
- **Revenue:** Shared revenue or referral fees

### 2. Co-Marketing

- **Goal:** Joint marketing campaigns
- **Benefit:** Expanded reach, credibility
- **Revenue:** Shared marketing costs

### 3. Referral Programs

- **Goal:** Partner referrals
- **Benefit:** Qualified leads
- **Revenue:** 20% revenue share

### 4. Technology Partnerships

- **Goal:** Technical integration
- **Benefit:** Better product, ecosystem
- **Revenue:** Mutual value

## Partnership Criteria

### Must Have

- Complementary product/service
- Shared target market
- Strong brand/reputation
- Willingness to co-market

### Nice to Have

- Existing customer base
- Technical integration possible
- Revenue sharing opportunity
- Strategic alignment
