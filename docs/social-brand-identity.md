# Social Brand Identity Consistency

## Brand Voice

### Tone
- **Professional but approachable**
- **Technical but not jargon-heavy**
- **Confident but not arrogant**
- **Helpful and supportive**

### Language
- Use "we" and "you" (conversational)
- Avoid corporate speak
- Be direct and clear
- Show, don't tell

## Visual Identity

### Colors
- **Primary:** Blue (#3B82F6)
- **Secondary:** Indigo (#6366F1)
- **Accent:** Green (#10B981) for success
- **Error:** Red (#EF4444)

### Typography
- **Headings:** Inter Bold
- **Body:** Inter Regular
- **Code:** JetBrains Mono

### Imagery
- Clean, modern, minimal
- Developer-focused
- Real screenshots preferred
- Consistent filter/editing style

## Content Guidelines

### Twitter/X
- **Frequency:** 3-5 posts per week
- **Content:** Tips, updates, community highlights
- **Hashtags:** #ReconciliationAPI #FinTech #DeveloperTools
- **Engagement:** Respond to all mentions within 24 hours

### LinkedIn
- **Frequency:** 2-3 posts per week
- **Content:** Thought leadership, case studies, company updates
- **Tone:** More professional than Twitter
- **Engagement:** Comment on relevant industry posts

### GitHub
- **Frequency:** As needed
- **Content:** Open source contributions, SDK updates
- **Tone:** Technical and precise
- **Engagement:** Respond to issues and PRs promptly

## Consistency Checklist

- [ ] Brand colors used correctly
- [ ] Typography consistent
- [ ] Voice and tone match guidelines
- [ ] Imagery style consistent
- [ ] Links and CTAs clear
- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors
- [ ] Mobile-friendly format
