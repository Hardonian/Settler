### SAML 2.0

1. Provide your IdP metadata

## RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)

Define custom roles with granular permissions:

- **Admin** - Full access
- **Developer** - API access, job management
- **Viewer** - Read-only access
- **Custom** - Define your own roles
