# Settler.dev Frontend Content & Messaging Overhaul

**Date:** January 2026  
**Purpose:** Comprehensive content rewrite to maximize clarity, conversion, credibility, and trust  
**Status:** Content Recommendations Complete

---

## Content Strategy Principles

1. **Clarity First:** Every message should be immediately understandable
2. **Benefit-Driven:** Focus on outcomes, not features
3. **Quantified:** Use specific numbers, percentages, and metrics
4. **Trust-Building:** Include social proof, credentials, and guarantees
5. **Action-Oriented:** Clear CTAs with compelling reasons to act
6. **Audience-Specific:** Tailor messaging to developers, finance teams, and executives

## Content Guidelines

### Voice & Tone
- **Professional but approachable**
- **Developer-friendly but business-aware**
- **Confident but not arrogant**
- **Helpful and educational**

### Writing Style
- Use active voice
- Short sentences (15-20 words)
- Simple language (avoid jargon when possible)
- Quantify everything (numbers, percentages, time)

### Messaging Hierarchy
1. **Benefit** (what user gets)
2. **Feature** (how it works)
3. **Technical detail** (for developers)
