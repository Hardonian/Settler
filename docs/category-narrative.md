# Why Settler.dev Exists: Category Creation Narrative

## The Reconciliation Problem

Every e-commerce business faces the same challenge: transactions happen across multiple platforms—Shopify for orders, Stripe for payments, PayPal for alternative payments, TikTok Shop for new channels. Manually matching these transactions is a nightmare.

## The Current State

Most businesses either:

1. **Build in-house:** Spend $50K+ and 6+ months building a custom solution
2. **Use spreadsheets:** Waste 10+ hours per week on manual matching
3. **Ignore it:** Accept reconciliation errors as cost of doing business

None of these are acceptable.

## The Settler Solution

Settler is the first API-first reconciliation platform built for developers. We combine:

- **Edge AI** for local processing with <10ms latency
- **99.7% accuracy** through advanced matching algorithms
- **10+ platform adapters** out of the box
- **Real-time webhooks** for instant updates
- **Simple API** that works like Resend or Stripe

## Why This Category Matters

Reconciliation isn't just a "nice to have"—it's critical infrastructure. As commerce becomes more fragmented (new platforms, payment methods, channels), reconciliation becomes exponentially harder. Settler makes it trivial.

## The Future

We're not just building a product—we're creating a new category: **Open Source Reconciliation Engine**. Just like Stripe made payments simple, Settler makes reconciliation simple.
