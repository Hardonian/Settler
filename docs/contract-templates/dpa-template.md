# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) - GDPR Template

## DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

**Pursuant to Article 28 of the GDPR**

### 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1. "Data Controller": Customer
1.2. "Data Processor": Settler
1.3. "Personal Data": As defined in GDPR Article 4

### 2. PROCESSING DETAILS

2.1. **Subject Matter:** Payment reconciliation services
2.2. **Duration:** Term of the Agreement
2.3. **Nature:** Automated processing of transaction data
2.4. **Purpose:** Reconciliation of financial transactions

### 3. PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS

3.1. Process Personal Data only on documented instructions.
3.2. Ensure confidentiality of Personal Data.
3.3. Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures.
3.4. Assist Controller with data subject rights requests.
3.5. Notify Controller of data breaches within 72 hours.

### 4. SECURITY MEASURES

4.1. Encryption in transit and at rest
4.2. Access controls and authentication
4.3. Regular security audits
4.4. Employee training on data protection

### 5. SUB-PROCESSORS

5.1. Processor may engage sub-processors with Controller approval.
5.2. List of approved sub-processors: [LIST]
5.3. Processor remains liable for sub-processor compliance.

### 6. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

6.1. Processor will assist Controller in responding to:
   - Right to access
   - Right to rectification
   - Right to erasure
   - Right to data portability
   - Right to object

### 7. DATA BREACH

7.1. Processor will notify Controller without undue delay.
7.2. Provide details of the breach and mitigation steps.

### 8. DATA RETENTION

8.1. Data retained only as long as necessary for services.
8.2. Deletion upon termination or Controller request.

### 9. AUDIT RIGHTS

9.1. Controller may audit Processor's compliance.
9.2. Audits subject to reasonable notice and confidentiality.

### 10. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

10.1. Transfers only to countries with adequate protection.
10.2. Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

---

**This template complies with GDPR Article 28 requirements.**
