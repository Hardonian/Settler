# Service Level Agreement (SLA) Template

## SETTLER SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

### 1. SERVICE LEVELS

#### 1.1 Uptime

- **Target:** 99.9% monthly uptime
- **Measurement:** Excludes scheduled maintenance
- **Credit:** 10% credit for each hour below 99.9%

#### 1.2 Response Time

- **Target:** 95% of API requests < 500ms
- **Measurement:** P95 latency
- **Credit:** 5% credit if below 95%

#### 1.3 Accuracy

- **Target:** 99.7% reconciliation accuracy
- **Measurement:** Correctly matched transactions
- **Credit:** 5% credit if below 99.5%

### 2. MAINTENANCE

2.1. Scheduled maintenance with 48 hours notice.
2.2. Emergency maintenance as needed.

### 3. SUPPORT

3.1. Email support: 24-hour response time
3.2. Critical issues: 4-hour response time (Enterprise)
3.3. Support hours: Business days, 9 AM - 5 PM PST

### 4. CREDITS

4.1. Credits applied to next invoice.
4.2. Maximum credit: 100% of monthly fee.
4.3. Must request credit within 30 days.

### 5. EXCLUSIONS

SLA does not apply to:

- Customer-caused issues
- Third-party service outages
- Force majeure events
- Beta/preview features
