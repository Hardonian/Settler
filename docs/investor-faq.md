# Investor FAQ

## Product & Market

### What problem does Settler solve?

Settler automates financial reconciliation across multiple e-commerce and payment platforms. Businesses waste 10+ hours per week manually matching transactions—Settler does this in minutes with 99.7% accuracy.

### What's your TAM?

Total Addressable Market: $2.5B (payment reconciliation software)  
Serviceable Addressable Market: $500M  
Serviceable Obtainable Market: $50M (10% of SAM)

### Who are your competitors?

- **Zapier:** General automation, not specialized for reconciliation
- **Supabase:** Backend-as-a-Service, requires custom development
- **In-house solutions:** Expensive ($50K+) and time-consuming (6+ months)

### What's your differentiation?

- **Edge AI:** Local processing with <10ms latency
- **99.7% accuracy:** Higher than general automation tools
- **Developer-first:** API design like Stripe/Resend
- **Pre-built adapters:** 10+ platforms out of the box
