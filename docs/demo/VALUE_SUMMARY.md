# Settler — Value Summary

**Five bullets. Copy into an email, slide, or intro message.**

---

- **Reconciliation in minutes, not days.** Settler automatically matches transactions across Stripe, your bank, and your accounting system on a schedule — bringing 20–30 hours/month of manual work down to under one hour.

- **Only exceptions reach your team.** The engine handles exact and fuzzy matching with confidence scoring. Your finance team sees only the records that genuinely need a decision, not everything.

- **One API for 50+ platforms.** Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, Amazon, QuickBooks, NetSuite, Xero, Plaid, and more — connected once, reconciled continuously.

- **Audit trail built in.** Every match, exception decision, export, and configuration change is logged with user, timestamp, and IP address — ready for compliance reviews without extra instrumentation.

- **Scales without additional headcount.** Once configured, processing 10x more transactions takes the same operational effort. Volume growth stops being a reconciliation problem.
