## Language Standards Established

### Replacements

| Old Language | New Language | Context |
|------------|--------------|---------|
| "eliminates" | "reduces" or "significantly reduces" | More accurate, less liability |
| "100%" | "high" or "99%+" (with context) | Acknowledges uncertainty |
| "guaranteed" | "target" or "SLA-backed (Enterprise)" | Accurate for non-Enterprise |
| "never" | "rarely" or "designed to minimize" | Realistic |
| "perfect" | "great" or "excellent" | Less absolute |
| "always" | "typically" or "consistently" | More accurate |
| "completely" | "largely" or "significantly" | Less absolute |

### Principles

1. **Honesty:** Acknowledge limitations, trust gaps, and constraints
2. **Specificity:** Use numbers, not vague claims
3. **Outcome-Focus:** Focus on outcomes, not technology
4. **Constraint-Aware:** Acknowledge what Settler does NOT do
5. **Trust-Building:** Address trust gaps explicitly
