## Personalization Tips

### Before Sending

1. **Research the Customer:**
   - Check their usage metrics
   - Note their specific achievements
   - Understand their use case

2. **Personalize:**
   - Use their name
   - Mention specific metrics
   - Reference their industry/use case

3. **Offer Value:**
   - Specific discount/benefit
   - Clear value proposition
   - Low time commitment

### After Sending

1. **Follow Up:**
   - Send follow-up if no response (after 1 week)
   - Be respectful of their time
   - Make it easy to say yes

2. **Thank You:**
   - Send thank you email after agreement
   - Deliver on promises (discount, support)
   - Keep them updated on publication
