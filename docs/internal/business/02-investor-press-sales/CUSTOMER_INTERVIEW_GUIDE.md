# Customer Interview Guide — Case Study Collection

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** January 2026  
**Purpose:** Guide for conducting customer interviews to collect case study material

---

## Interview Overview

**Duration:** 15-30 minutes  
**Format:** Video call (Zoom, Google Meet)  
**Goal:** Collect metrics, quotes, and stories for case study

---

## Pre-Interview Preparation

### Research the Customer

1. **Review Customer Profile:**
   - Company name, industry, size
   - Usage metrics (transactions/month, reconciliations/month)
   - Time using Settler
   - Specific achievements

2. **Prepare Questions:**
   - Customize questions based on their use case
   - Prepare follow-up questions
   - Have metrics ready to discuss

3. **Schedule:**
   - Send calendar invite
   - Include agenda
   - Send reminder 24 hours before

---

## Interview Structure

### Opening (2-3 minutes)

**Goals:**
- Build rapport
- Set expectations
- Explain purpose

**Script:**
"Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time to talk with me today! I really appreciate you being willing to share your Settler experience.

Just to set expectations, this will be a quick 15-30 minute conversation. I'll ask you some questions about your experience with Settler, and we'll use this to create a case study that showcases your success.

Everything we discuss will be shared with you for approval before we publish anything. Sound good?"

### The Challenge (5-7 minutes)

**Goal:** Understand their pain points before Settler

**Questions:**
1. "What was your reconciliation process like before Settler?"
2. "How much time were you spending on reconciliation?"
3. "What were the main pain points or challenges?"
4. "Were there any specific errors or issues you were experiencing?"
5. "How was this affecting your team or business?"

**Follow-up Questions:**
- "Can you give me a specific example?"
- "How often did this happen?"
- "What was the impact on your team?"

### The Solution (5-7 minutes)

**Goal:** Understand why they chose Settler and how they implemented it

**Questions:**
1. "What made you decide to try Settler?"
2. "How did you hear about Settler?"
3. "What was the implementation process like?"
4. "How long did it take to get up and running?"
5. "What was the time to value?"

**Follow-up Questions:**
- "What was the easiest part?"
- "What was the most challenging part?"
- "Did you need any support during implementation?"

### The Results (5-10 minutes)

**Goal:** Collect specific metrics and outcomes

**Questions:**
1. "What results have you seen since using Settler?"
2. "How much time are you saving now?"
3. "Have you seen improvements in accuracy?"
4. "What about cost savings?"
5. "How has this impacted your team?"
6. "Any other benefits you've noticed?"

**Metrics to Collect:**
- Time savings (hours/month)
- Accuracy improvements (%)
- Cost savings ($/month)
- Transaction volume (transactions/month)
- Error reduction (%)
- Time to value (days)

**Follow-up Questions:**
- "Can you quantify that?"
- "What was it before vs. after?"
- "How do you measure success?"

### The Quote (2-3 minutes)

**Goal:** Get a compelling testimonial quote

**Questions:**
1. "If you had to summarize your Settler experience in one sentence, what would you say?"
2. "What would you tell someone who's considering Settler?"
3. "What's the biggest benefit you've seen?"
4. "How would you describe Settler to a colleague?"

**Tips:**
- Ask for specific, measurable benefits
- Ask for emotional impact
- Ask for comparison (before vs. after)

### Closing (2-3 minutes)

**Goal:** Set expectations and next steps

**Script:**
"Thank you so much for sharing your experience! This is really helpful.

**Next steps:**
1. I'll draft a case study based on our conversation
2. I'll send it to you for review and approval
3. Once approved, we'll publish it on our website
4. You'll receive [discount/benefit] as a thank you

**Timeline:**
- Draft: [Date]
- Your review: [Date]
- Publication: [Date]

Do you have any questions? Is there anything else you'd like to add?"

## Post-Interview Actions

### Immediately After

1. **Send Thank You:**
   - Thank them for their time
   - Confirm next steps
   - Send [discount/benefit] if applicable

2. **Transcribe:**
   - Transcribe interview (if recorded)
   - Review notes
   - Identify key quotes

3. **Draft Case Study:**
   - Write case study using template
   - Include metrics and quotes
   - Get approval before publishing

### Within 1 Week

1. **Send Draft:**
   - Send case study draft for review
   - Request approval
   - Make any requested changes

2. **Finalize:**
   - Get final approval
   - Publish case study
   - Share with customer

## Tips for Success

### During Interview

1. **Listen Actively:**
   - Let them tell their story
   - Don't interrupt
   - Ask follow-up questions

2. **Be Specific:**
   - Ask for numbers
   - Ask for examples
   - Ask for comparisons

3. **Be Respectful:**
   - Respect their time
   - Don't push if uncomfortable
   - Thank them for sharing

### After Interview

1. **Follow Up Quickly:**
   - Send thank you within 24 hours
   - Send draft within 1 week
   - Deliver on promises

2. **Get Approval:**
   - Always get approval before publishing
   - Make requested changes
   - Respect their preferences
