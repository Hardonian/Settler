## Immediate Actions (0-1 month)

### 1. Collect Customer Success Stories (Week 1-2)

**Owner:** Customer Success / Sales  
**Status:** 🔴 Not Started

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Identify 5-10 customers with positive outcomes
- [ ] Reach out to customers for permission to share stories
- [ ] Collect quantitative results (time saved, cost savings, accuracy improvements)
- [ ] Collect qualitative feedback (quotes, testimonials)
- [ ] Document success metrics

**Deliverables:**
- List of 5-10 customer success stories (internal)
- Customer permission forms
- Success metrics database

**Timeline:** 2 weeks

**Outreach Template:**
```
Subject: Share Your Settler Success Story?

Hi [Customer Name],

We're thrilled that Settler has helped [specific outcome]. 

Would you be open to sharing your story? We'd love to:
- Create a case study (with your approval)
- Feature your logo on our website
- Use a quote in our marketing materials

We can make it anonymous if you prefer, or use your company name with your permission.

Let me know if you're interested!

Best,
[Your Name]
```
