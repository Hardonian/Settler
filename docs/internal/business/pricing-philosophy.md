# Settler Pricing Philosophy: One-Page Summary

**How Settler Charges and Why**

---

## Core Principle

**Settler charges based on risk removed, compliance guaranteed, and operational certainty provided—not just transaction volume.**

We eliminate existential risk ($100K+ annual cost), not just inconvenience. Our pricing reflects the value we deliver, not the features we provide.

---

## Value-Based Pricing Axes

1. **Reconciliation Volume** (Primary)
   - Correlates directly with value delivered
   - Current: 1K-1M/month across tiers
   - Recommendation: Keep as primary axis

2. **Compliance Level** (Secondary)
   - Eliminates existential risk
   - Current: Not explicitly monetized
   - Recommendation: Growth+ only ($299+)

3. **Automation Level** (Tertiary)
   - Eliminates manual work
   - Current: Starter+ only
   - Recommendation: Keep enforcement

4. **Support Level** (Quaternary)
   - Operational certainty
   - Current: Varies by tier
   - Recommendation: Keep differentiation

---

## Recommended Pricing Structure

| Tier | Price | Reconciliations | Compliance | Automation | Support |
|------|-------|------------------|------------|------------|---------|
| **Starter** | $99/month | 10,000/month | Basic audit trails | Scheduled jobs | Email (24h) |
| **Growth** | $299/month | 100,000/month | SOC 2 ready, 1-year retention | Unlimited scheduled | Priority (4h) |
| **Enterprise** | Custom ($2K-10K/month) | Unlimited | 7-year retention, certifications | Everything | Dedicated (1h) |

**Changes:**
- Eliminated Free tier → 14-day free trial
- Eliminated Scale tier → Merged into Growth
- Increased prices → 3-5x current pricing
- Enforcement-based → Tiers reflect risk reduction

---

## What Is Intentionally Expensive

1. **Compliance Features** ($299+)
   - Eliminates $100K+ annual risk
   - Finance Directors willing to pay $500-2,000/month
   - Still 10-50x cheaper than alternatives

2. **Enterprise Tier** (Custom $2K-10K/month)
   - Dedicated support, custom compliance
   - CFOs willing to pay $5K-20K/month
   - Still 10-50x cheaper than BlackLine

3. **Custom Adapters** ($1,000 setup + $100/month)
   - High maintenance cost, custom work
   - Still cheaper than custom code ($20K-80K/year)

---

## What Is Intentionally Cheap or Free

1. **14-Day Free Trial**
   - Reduces barrier to entry
   - Creates habit formation
   - Standard SaaS practice

2. **Unlimited Users**
   - Team-based pricing causes churn
   - Doesn't correlate with value
   - Differentiates from competitors

3. **Webhooks Included**
   - Core to value proposition
   - Charging would undermine value
   - Infrastructure cost, not value driver

4. **Exceptions Included**
   - Charging creates adversarial relationship
   - Part of value proposition (we identify problems)
   - Never charge per exception

## Final Check: "If Settler Doubled Its Prices Tomorrow"

**Finance Directors / CFOs:** ✅ **YES, WOULD DEFINITELY STILL PAY**
- Eliminates $100K+ annual risk (14x ROI)
- Doubled price ($598/month) still within willingness to pay ($500-2,000/month)

**CTOs / VP Engineering:** ✅ **YES, WOULD LIKELY STILL PAY**
- Eliminates $50K+ annual maintenance (7x ROI)
- Doubled price ($598/month) still within willingness to pay ($200-1,000/month)

**Operations Managers:** ⚠️ **MAYBE, WOULD NEED JUSTIFICATION**
- Doubled price ($198/month) might exceed willingness to pay
- Would need strong ROI justification

**Conclusion:** Settler can **definitely double prices** for Finance Directors and CTOs. Operations Managers would need justification but would likely advocate given strong ROI.
