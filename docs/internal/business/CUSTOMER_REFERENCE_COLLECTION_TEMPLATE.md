# Customer Reference Collection Template

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** January 2026  
**Status:** Active  
**Purpose:** Template for collecting and publishing customer references

---

## Collection Process

### Step 1: Identify Candidates (Week 1)

**Criteria for Good References:**
- ✅ Positive outcomes (time saved, cost savings, accuracy improvements)
- ✅ Quantifiable results (specific metrics)
- ✅ Willing to share story (with permission)
- ✅ Representative use case (matches target customer profile)

**Sources:**
- Customer success metrics (usage, retention, expansion)
- Support tickets (positive feedback)
- Sales calls (customer mentions success)
- Product usage (high engagement, growth)

**Action:**
- [ ] Review customer database for candidates
- [ ] Identify 5-10 potential references
- [ ] Check customer health scores
- [ ] Verify customer is active and satisfied

---

### Step 2: Outreach (Week 1-2)

**Email Template:**

```
Subject: Share Your Settler Success Story?

Hi [Customer Name],

We're thrilled that Settler has helped [specific outcome - e.g., "save 20 hours per week on reconciliation"].

Would you be open to sharing your story? We'd love to:
- Create a case study (with your approval)
- Feature your logo on our website
- Use a quote in our marketing materials

We can make it anonymous if you prefer, or use your company name with your permission.

This helps other companies like yours understand how Settler can help them too.

Let me know if you're interested!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Follow-up:**
- [ ] Send initial email
- [ ] Follow up after 3-5 days if no response
- [ ] Offer incentives if needed (discount, extended trial, etc.)

---

### Step 3: Collect Information (Week 2-3)

**Interview Questions:**

**Company Background:**
1. What does your company do?
2. What industry are you in?
3. How many employees?
4. What's your annual revenue (if comfortable sharing)?

**Problem:**
5. What problem were you trying to solve?
6. How were you handling reconciliation before Settler?
7. What pain points did you experience?
8. How much time/money was this costing you?

**Solution:**
9. Why did you choose Settler?
10. What other options did you consider?
11. What was the implementation process like?
12. How long did it take to set up?

**Results:**
13. What results have you seen? (quantify)
14. How much time do you save?
15. How much money have you saved?
16. What's improved? (accuracy, compliance, etc.)
17. What would you say to someone considering Settler?

**Permission:**
18. Can we use your company name?
19. Can we use your logo?
20. Can we quote you?
21. Can we use specific metrics?

---

### Step 4: Create Case Study (Week 3-4)

**Case Study Template:**

```markdown
# Case Study: [Company Name] Saves [X Hours/Week] with Settler

**Company:** [Company Name]  
**Industry:** [Industry]  
**Use Case:** [Use Case]  
**Results:** [Key Results]

## Executive Summary

[Company Name] is a [description]. Before Settler, [problem]. After implementing Settler, [solution and results].

**Key Results:**
- ⏱️ **Time Saved:** [X hours/week]
- 💰 **Cost Savings:** $[X]/month
- ✅ **Accuracy:** [X]% (up from [Y]%)
- 🚀 **Implementation Time:** [X days]

## The Challenge

[Problem description - 2-3 paragraphs]

## The Solution

[Why Settler - 2-3 paragraphs]

## The Results

[Results - 2-3 paragraphs with metrics]

## Customer Quote

> "[Quote]"
> — [Name], [Title], [Company Name]

## Conclusion

[Summary - 1 paragraph]
```

**Action:**
- [ ] Write case study draft
- [ ] Send to customer for approval
- [ ] Incorporate feedback
- [ ] Finalize case study

---

### Step 5: Publish (Week 4)

**Publishing Checklist:**
- [ ] Customer approved case study
- [ ] Logo permission obtained (if using logo)
- [ ] Quote permission obtained
- [ ] Metrics verified
- [ ] Published on website
- [ ] Added to customer success page
- [ ] Shared on social media
- [ ] Added to sales materials
