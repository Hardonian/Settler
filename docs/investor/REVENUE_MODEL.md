# Revenue Model & Expansion Vectors

## Revenue Model

### Primary Revenue: Usage-Based SaaS

**Tiered Pricing with Overage:**

1. **Free Tier**
   - 100 recon comparisons/month
   - 1 concurrent job
   - Community support
   - **Purpose:** Developer acquisition, product-led growth

2. **Starter - $99/month**
   - 10,000 recon comparisons/month
   - 5 concurrent jobs
   - Email support
   - **Target:** SMB, startups

3. **Pro - $499/month**
   - 100,000 recon comparisons/month
   - 20 concurrent jobs
   - Priority support
   - **Target:** Mid-market, growing companies

4. **Business - $1,999/month**
   - 1,000,000 recon comparisons/month
   - 100 concurrent jobs
   - Dedicated support
   - **Target:** Enterprise, high-volume

5. **Enterprise - Custom**
   - Unlimited usage
   - 1,000+ concurrent jobs
   - SLA guarantee
   - Custom integrations
   - **Target:** Large enterprises

### Overage Pricing

- Recon comparisons: $0.01 per 1,000
- Validations: $0.005 per 1,000
- Transformations: $0.01 per 1,000
- Mappings: $0.005 per 1,000
- AI tokens: Varies by model ($0.002-$0.03 per 1K tokens)
- Storage: $0.10 per GB/month
- Webhooks: $0.001 per webhook

### Revenue Projections

**Year 1:**

- 100 customers @ $50K ARR average = $5M ARR
- Mix: 50% Starter, 30% Pro, 20% Business

**Year 2:**

- 500 customers @ $60K ARR average = $30M ARR
- Mix: 40% Starter, 35% Pro, 25% Business

**Year 3:**

- 1,000 customers @ $70K ARR average = $70M ARR
- Mix: 30% Starter, 40% Pro, 30% Business/Enterprise

## Expansion Vectors

### 1. Vertical Module Add-Ons

**Revenue Stream:** Additional monthly fees for vertical modules

- LegalTech Module: +$199/month
- FinTech Module: +$299/month
- EdTech Module: +$149/month
- Compliance Module: +$249/month

**Projection:** 30% of customers purchase 1+ vertical module = $2M ARR (Year 2)

### 2. Enterprise Services

**Revenue Stream:** Professional services, custom integrations

- Implementation: $10K-$50K one-time
- Custom integrations: $25K-$100K one-time
- Training: $5K-$20K per engagement

**Projection:** 20% of Enterprise customers = $5M services revenue (Year 2)

### 3. Marketplace Revenue Share

**Revenue Stream:** 20% commission on marketplace transactions

- Template sales: $10-$100 per template
- Workflow sales: $50-$500 per workflow
- Plugin sales: $100-$1,000 per plugin

**Projection:** $1M GMV = $200K revenue (Year 2)

### 4. Usage-Based Expansion

**Revenue Stream:** Natural expansion as customers grow

- More reconciliations = higher tier
- More workflows = more usage
- More integrations = more data

**Projection:** 30% net revenue retention (Year 2)

### 5. AI Token Revenue

**Revenue Stream:** Markup on AI model usage

- Cost: $0.002-$0.03 per 1K tokens
- Price: $0.003-$0.04 per 1K tokens
- Margin: 20-30%

**Projection:** $2M AI token revenue (Year 2)

## Unit Economics

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):**

- SMB: $500
- Mid-market: $5,000
- Enterprise: $50,000

**Lifetime Value (LTV):**

- SMB: $5,000 (5 years)
- Mid-market: $50,000 (5 years)
- Enterprise: $500,000 (5 years)

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**

- SMB: 10:1
- Mid-market: 10:1
- Enterprise: 10:1

**Payback Period:**

- SMB: 6 months
- Mid-market: 12 months
- Enterprise: 18 months
