## Why I'm Building Settler

1. **The Pain is Universal:** Every scaling business I've worked at or advised hits a wall where the finance team is spending days manually matching Stripe payouts to database records in Excel.
2. **Current Solutions are Flawed:** Existing tools are either prohibitively expensive (BlackLine) or not built for reconciliation (Zapier).
3. **The Architecture is Obvious:** Reconciliation isn't an AI problem; it's a determinism and normalization problem. By building an adapter-first system with a Rust kernel, we can solve this at the root.
