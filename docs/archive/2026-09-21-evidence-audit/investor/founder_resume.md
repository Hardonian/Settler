## Why I'm Building Settler

1. **Manual reconciliation is still a hidden tax on growth.** Finance and operations teams burn time stitching together partial truths from processors, ledgers, and internal systems.
2. **Most products solve the reporting layer, not the system truth layer.** Settler is aimed at normalized evidence, deterministic reconciliation, and operator trust.
3. **My edge is architecture plus execution.** I understand enterprise workflow adoption, but I also build the systems that make those workflows operable and defensible.
