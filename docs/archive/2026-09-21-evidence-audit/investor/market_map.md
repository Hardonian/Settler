## Market Dynamics

### Why Enterprise Incumbents Won't Move Down-Market

1. **Margin structure:** Enterprise vendors earn $77K–$340K/yr per customer. Pursuing $29/month customers would destroy their unit economics.
2. **Sales motion:** Enterprise sales cycles are 3–6 months with implementation teams. Self-service PLG contradicts their GTM.
3. **Architecture:** Built for on-premise or private cloud. Not serverless-first.
4. **Feature bloat:** Close management, journal entry, task management — not reconciliation-native.

### Why General Automation Won't Add Reconciliation

1. **Horizontal focus:** Zapier/Make/Tray serve ALL use cases. Adding deep reconciliation is a category pivot.
2. **No matching engine:** Moving data between apps ≠ matching transactions across sources with tolerance rules.
3. **No evidence generation:** Proofpacks require deterministic computation and hash linking — outside automation platform architecture.

### Settler's Window

The mid-market reconciliation gap exists because:

- Enterprise tools are too expensive and too heavy
- Automation tools don't do reconciliation
- Spreadsheets don't scale or generate evidence
- **Nobody has built a reconciliation-native, evidence-generating, mid-market SaaS product**

That's Settler's wedge.
