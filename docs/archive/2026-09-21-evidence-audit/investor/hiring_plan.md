# Settler — Hiring Plan (Next 12 Months)

**Purpose:** Due diligence material detailing post-funding hiring strategy
**Last Updated:** June 2026
**Assumes:** $250K - $500K Pre-Seed Raise

---

## Hiring Philosophy

1. **Keep the team lean:** The core product is already built and production-ready. We do not need a large engineering team to find PMF.
2. **Hire for leverage, not capacity:** Every early hire must be a force multiplier (e.g., someone who can build the next 50 adapters, or someone who can handle all GTM content and support).
3. **No premature scaling:** We will not hire dedicated sales reps until the founder has closed the first 50 customers and validated the sales motion.

## Phase 1: Post-Raise to PMF (Months 1–6)

**Goal:** Secure the first 50 paying customers and validate the product-market fit.
**Headcount Target:** 1 Founder + 1 FTE (or 2 Part-time/Contractors)

### Hire 1: Founding Full-Stack Engineer

- **Target Start:** Month 2
- **Profile:** Senior generalist, highly autonomous. Experience with Next.js, TypeScript, and ideally Rust.
- **Primary Responsibilities:**
  1. Build and maintain the next batch of platform adapters (ERP, HRIS, banking).
  2. Implement customer feature requests from the pilot program.
  3. Manage bug fixes and operational maintenance, freeing the founder to focus on sales and GTM.
- **Compensation Structure:** Market rate base salary + significant early-stage equity (0.5% - 2.0%).
- **Why this role:** Founder must transition from 90% coding to 70% sales/marketing. This hire ensures product momentum doesn't stall.

### Hire 2 (Optional/Contract): Growth Marketer / Content

- **Target Start:** Month 3 (Contract/Fractional)
- **Profile:** Content creator with deep understanding of finance/ops workflows.
- **Primary Responsibilities:**
  1. Write detailed SEO content (e.g., "How to reconcile Stripe payouts with Xero").
  2. Manage distribution across LinkedIn, Reddit, and communities.
  3. Create product documentation and tutorials.
- **Compensation Structure:** Retainer or hourly rate.
- **Why this role:** Scales top-of-funnel awareness without committing to a full-time salary before PMF is validated.

---

## Phase 2: Scaling Post-PMF (Months 7–12)

**Goal:** Scale from 50 to 200 paying customers, prepare for Seed round.
**Headcount Target:** 3–4 FTEs

### Hire 3: Customer Success / Sales Engineer

- **Target Start:** Month 7
- **Profile:** Technical enough to help customers map their CSVs or configure API connections, empathetic enough to run onboarding.
- **Primary Responsibilities:**
  1. Take over customer onboarding from the founder.
  2. Run the pilot program motion.
  3. Manage priority support and document feature requests.
  4. Expand usage within existing accounts.
- **Compensation Structure:** Base salary + performance bonus tied to retention and expansion.
- **Why this role:** At 50+ customers, founder-led onboarding becomes a bottleneck. This role protects retention and drives expansion revenue.

### Hire 4: Founding Account Executive

- **Target Start:** Month 9
- **Profile:** Consultative seller, experience selling technical products to finance/ops teams.
- **Primary Responsibilities:**
  1. Take over the founder's validated sales playbook.
  2. Run outbound campaigns to target ICPs.
  3. Close deals in the Growth and Professional tiers.
- **Compensation Structure:** Base salary + commission (OTE structure).
- **Why this role:** Only hired AFTER the founder has proven the sales motion is repeatable and scalable.
