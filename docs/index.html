Frequently Asked Questions

What payment rails do you support?
Stripe, PayPal, bank transfers (CSV/OFX), local PSPs, and any custom format you can export as structured data. If it has transaction ID, amount, and date — Settler can match it. Custom rail support included in Growth and Scale tiers.
How does TigerBeetle make reconciliation better?
TigerBeetle is a deterministic accounting ledger designed for financial transactions. Every transfer is recorded with cryptographic integrity — you can verify that no transaction was altered after the fact. This gives your auditors tamper-evident proof of every match, exception, and override.
How long does setup take?
Upload your first CSV and configure matching rules in under 30 minutes. Most teams see their first match results in the same session. Our pilot program includes a 45-minute setup call to configure custom rules for your specific payment rails and exception handling needs.
Can Settler replace our ERP?
No — and it's not designed to. Settler is a specialized reconciliation engine that sits alongside your ERP. You export transaction data, run matching in Settler, and export proofpacks back to your accounting system. No integration required, no data migration risk.
What if we need more than 250K matching runs?
The Scale tier supports up to 250K runs/month. If you need more, we offer custom enterprise plans with unlimited runs, dedicated infrastructure, and SLA commitments. Contact our sales team to discuss your volume.
