# Acquisition-Grade Execution Plan

Acquisition is an outcome, not the operating objective. The objective is to build an independent
business with a capability a strategic buyer cannot cheaply reproduce.

## Days 0–30: focus and first commitments

- Freeze speculative feature work outside the canonical reconciliation path.
- Select one ICP: vertical software or finance platforms with multiple processors and ledgers.
- Select one workflow: processor payout to bank deposit to ledger, including fees, refunds,
  disputes, and FX.
- Complete 20 discovery interviews using recorded problem and buying evidence.
- Secure three paid design-partner commitments.
- Assign real people to engineering, commercial, security, and legal accountabilities.

Exit criterion: signed commercial evidence exists for at least one production-bound pilot.

## Days 31–90: production truth

- Put the canonical workflow into production for the first customer.
- Capture the scorecard defined in `CUSTOMER_PROOF.md`.
- Demonstrate replay, evidence export, tenant isolation, failure recovery, and rollback.
- Remove founder-only onboarding steps or document them as services work.
- Complete contributor/IP-assignment and dependency-license audits.

Exit criterion: one paying production customer with reproducible baseline and outcome evidence.

## Months 4–9: repeatability

- Reach five to ten paying production customers.
- Obtain three referenceable customers with written consent.
- Show repeatable onboarding for the same workflow and ICP.
- Measure retention, implementation cost, gross margin, and customer concentration monthly.
- Complete an independent penetration test and remediate high-severity findings.
- Establish one relevant platform partnership or marketplace listing.

Exit criterion: credible product-market evidence and an enterprise diligence packet.

## Months 10–18: strategic leverage

- Demonstrate a permissioned exception corpus that improves resolution quality or onboarding speed.
- Establish meaningful reconciled volume and documented customer dependence.
- Maintain source-backed ARR, NRR, margin, and concentration history.
- Win joint customers involving a plausible strategic acquirer.
- Develop product-team relationships before any corporate-development outreach.

Exit criterion: an acquirer can quantify build-versus-buy advantage from customer, data, team, and
time-to-market evidence.

## Weekly founder scoreboard

1. Qualified customer conversations.
2. Paid pilots signed.
3. Production customers live.
4. Referenceable customers.
5. Reconciled financial and record volume.
6. Customer outcome packets completed.
7. Onboarding days and founder hours per implementation.
8. Open security and diligence blockers.

Repository feature count is not a company KPI.
