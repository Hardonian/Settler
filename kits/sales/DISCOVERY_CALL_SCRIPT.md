# Discovery Call Script

**Last Updated:** 2025-01-20  
**Status:** Revenue Bootstrap - Active  
**Purpose:** Structured discovery call script for sales conversations

## Overview

This script helps you:
- **Understand the problem:** What are they trying to solve?
- **Qualify the prospect:** Do they meet qualification criteria?
- **Build rapport:** Establish trust and credibility
- **Move to next step:** Demo, pilot, or disqualify

**Philosophy:** Discovery before demo. Understand before selling.

---

## Call Structure (30 minutes)

### Opening (5 minutes)
**Goal:** Build rapport, set agenda, understand context

**Script:**
"Thanks for your time today. I'd like to understand your reconciliation challenges and see if Settler might be a good fit. Does that work for you?"

**Questions:**
- "What's your role at [Company]?"
- "What does your team do?"
- "What's your current reconciliation process?"

---

### Problem Discovery (10 minutes)
**Goal:** Understand the problem, quantify the pain

**Questions:**

1. **Current Process**
   - "What's your current reconciliation process?"
   - "How long does reconciliation take?"
   - "How many people are involved?"

2. **Pain Points**
   - "What's the biggest challenge with reconciliation?"
   - "What errors or issues do you encounter?"
   - "What happens when reconciliation doesn't match?"

3. **Quantification**
   - "How many transactions do you process per month?"
   - "How many hours per month does reconciliation take?"
   - "What's your error rate?"
   - "What's the cost of errors or manual work?"

**Listen for:**
- Time spent (2+ days/month = qualified)
- Error rate (5%+ = qualified)
- Manual work (spreadsheets, manual matching = qualified)
- Multi-system reconciliation (Stripe + Shopify + QuickBooks = qualified)

---

### Use Case Discovery (5 minutes)
**Goal:** Understand specific use case, systems involved

**Questions:**

1. **Systems**
   - "What systems do you need to reconcile between?"
   - "Do you use Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks, PayPal, Xero?"
   - "Do you have API access to these systems?"

2. **Volume**
   - "How many transactions per month?"
   - "Do you have multi-currency transactions?"
   - "What's your growth trajectory?"

3. **Requirements**
   - "What features are must-haves?"
   - "What integrations do you need?"
   - "Any compliance or security requirements?"

**Listen for:**
- Multiple systems (qualified)
- API access available (qualified)
- 10K+ transactions/month (qualified)
- Standard integrations sufficient (qualified)

---

### Authority & Budget Discovery (5 minutes)
**Goal:** Understand decision-making process, budget

**Questions:**

1. **Authority**
   - "Who makes budget decisions for tools like this?"
   - "Are you the decision maker, or do you need approval?"
   - "Who else needs to be involved?"

2. **Budget**
   - "What's your budget for reconciliation tools?"
   - "How do you justify tool purchases? (ROI, cost savings?)"
   - "What would make this a no-brainer purchase?"

3. **Timeline**
   - "When do you need this solved?"
   - "What's your timeline for making a decision?"
   - "Any upcoming events or deadlines?"

**Listen for:**
- Decision maker involved (qualified)
- Budget confirmed ($100-$500/month = qualified)
- Timeline confirmed (within 30 days = qualified)

---

### Qualification Check (3 minutes)
**Goal:** Confirm qualification, identify any gaps

**Qualification Checklist:**
- ✅ Problem clarity (clear problem, quantifiable pain)
- ✅ Data readiness (API access, sample data)
- ✅ Authority (decision maker involved)
- ✅ Timeline (within 30 days)
- ✅ Budget signal ($100-$500/month)

**If Qualified:**
"Based on what you've shared, Settler sounds like a great fit. Would you like to see a demo?"

**If Not Qualified:**
"I appreciate your time. Based on what you've shared, Settler may not be the right fit right now. [Disqualify politely, reconnect later]"

---

### Next Steps (2 minutes)
**Goal:** Set clear next steps, confirm commitment

**If Qualified:**
"Great. Here's what I'd like to propose:
1. **Demo:** 30-minute demo showing Settler in action
2. **Pilot:** 14-day pilot, no credit card required
3. **Follow-up:** Check-in after demo to discuss pilot

Does that work for you?"

**If Not Qualified:**
"Thanks for your time. I'll reconnect in [timeframe] to see if your situation has changed."

## Discovery Questions by ICP

### E-commerce Finance Manager
- "How long does monthly reconciliation take?"
- "What's your error rate between Stripe and QuickBooks?"
- "How many transactions do you process per month?"

### SaaS Operations Lead
- "Do you have multi-currency transactions?"
- "How do you handle currency conversion?"
- "What's your reconciliation process for Stripe + PayPal?"

### Accounting Firm Partner
- "How many clients do you reconcile for?"
- "How long does reconciliation take per client?"
- "What's your margin on reconciliation work?"

### Fintech Operations Manager
- "What compliance requirements do you have?"
- "Do you need audit trails for reconciliation?"
- "What certifications do you need?"

### E-commerce Developer/Founder
- "Are you building reconciliation logic yourself?"
- "How much time do you spend on reconciliation code?"
- "What bugs or issues have you encountered?"

## Red Flags (Disqualify)

### Volume Too Low
- <1K transactions/month
- Response: "Settler is designed for companies doing 10K+ transactions/month. I'd be happy to reconnect when you're processing more volume."

### Wrong Use Case
- Only one system (no multi-system reconciliation)
- Response: "Settler is designed for companies reconciling between multiple systems. If you're only using one system, you might be better served by that system's native features."

### No Budget Authority
- No budget, or needs approval from others
- Response: "Settler requires budget approval. I'd be happy to connect with your finance/operations team who has budget authority."

### Custom Requirements
- Extensive custom development needed
- Response: "Settler is a SaaS platform with standard integrations. If you need extensive custom development, we're not the right fit."

## Objection Handling During Discovery

### "We don't have API access"
**Response:** "Settler requires API access to your systems. If you don't have API access, we can help you set it up. If API access isn't possible, you might be better served by manual reconciliation tools."

### "We're too small"
**Response:** "Settler is designed for companies doing 10K+ transactions/month. If you're smaller, manual reconciliation may be sufficient. However, if you're growing, it might make sense to start now."

### "We already have a process"
**Response:** "If your current process works, great. Settler is for companies where reconciliation takes 2+ days/month and is error-prone. If that's not you, we may not be the right fit."

## Post-Call Actions

### If Qualified
1. **Send demo invite:** Email with calendar link
2. **Prepare demo:** Review use case, prepare specific demo
3. **Set reminder:** Follow up if no response in 2 days

### If Not Qualified
1. **Disqualify politely:** Send polite rejection email
2. **Document reason:** Note why disqualified
3. **Set reconnect:** Schedule reconnect in 3-6 months

### Always
1. **Send thank you:** Thank you email within 24 hours
2. **Document notes:** Record call notes in CRM
3. **Update pipeline:** Update pipeline status
