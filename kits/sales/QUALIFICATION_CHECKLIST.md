# Qualification Checklist

**Last Updated:** 2025-01-20  
**Status:** Revenue Bootstrap - Active  
**Purpose:** Quick qualification checklist for sales conversations

## Overview

Use this checklist during discovery calls to quickly qualify prospects. Score each criterion (1 point if qualified, 0 if disqualified). Total score determines qualification level.

**Philosophy:** Better to disqualify early than waste time on bad fits.

---

## Qualification Criteria

### 1. Problem Clarity (Required)

**Question:** "What problem are you trying to solve?"

**Qualify if:**

- ✅ Clear problem statement (manual reconciliation, errors, time-consuming)
- ✅ Specific use case (Stripe + Shopify + QuickBooks reconciliation)
- ✅ Quantifiable pain (2+ days/month, 5%+ error rate)

**Disqualify if:**

- ❌ Vague problem ("we need better reconciliation")
- ❌ Wrong use case (single system, no multi-system reconciliation)
- ❌ No quantifiable pain ("it's fine, just slow")

**Score:** 1 point if qualified, 0 if disqualified

---

### 2. Data Readiness (Required)

**Question:** "Do you have API access to your systems?"

**Qualify if:**

- ✅ API access available (Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks APIs)
- ✅ Sample data available (at least 1K transactions)
- ✅ Technical capability (can integrate APIs)

**Disqualify if:**

- ❌ No API access (manual data entry only)
- ❌ No sample data (can't test)
- ❌ No technical capability (can't integrate)

**Score:** 1 point if qualified, 0 if disqualified

---

### 3. Authority (Required)

**Question:** "Who makes budget decisions for tools like this?"

**Qualify if:**

- ✅ Decision maker involved (Finance Manager, CFO, Operations Lead)
- ✅ Budget authority confirmed ($100-$500/month approval)
- ✅ Timeline confirmed (decision within 30 days)

**Disqualify if:**

- ❌ No decision maker (individual contributor, no budget)
- ❌ No budget authority (needs approval from others)
- ❌ Long timeline (>90 days, no urgency)

**Score:** 1 point if qualified, 0 if disqualified

---

### 4. Timeline (Required)

**Question:** "When do you need this solved?"

**Qualify if:**

- ✅ Urgent (within 30 days)
- ✅ Clear timeline (specific date or event)
- ✅ Budget approved or pending (likely to approve)

**Disqualify if:**

- ❌ No urgency ("someday", "exploring options")
- ❌ Long timeline (>90 days)
- ❌ No budget approved (unlikely to approve)

**Score:** 1 point if qualified, 0 if disqualified

---

### 5. Budget Signal (Required)

**Question:** "What's your budget for reconciliation tools?"

**Qualify if:**

- ✅ Budget confirmed ($100-$500/month)
- ✅ Budget approved or pending (likely to approve)
- ✅ ROI understood (saves $2K-$5K/month in labor)

**Disqualify if:**

- ❌ No budget ($0, expecting free)
- ❌ Budget too low (<$50/month)
- ❌ Budget unclear (don't know, need to check)

**Score:** 1 point if qualified, 0 if disqualified

---

## Qualification Scoring

### Scoring System

- **Total Points:** 5 points maximum (1 point per criterion)
- **Qualification Threshold:** 4+ points (meet 4+ criteria)

### Qualification Levels

- **Highly Qualified (5 points):** All criteria met → Fast-track to pilot
- **Qualified (4 points):** Most criteria met → Standard pilot process
- **Marginally Qualified (3 points):** Some criteria met → Extended qualification
- **Disqualified (<3 points):** Few criteria met → Disqualify, don't waste time

## Quick Qualification Script

### Opening

"Thanks for your time. I'd like to understand your reconciliation challenges and see if Settler might be a good fit. Does that work for you?"

### Problem Discovery

"What's your current reconciliation process? How long does it take? What's your biggest challenge?"

### Use Case Discovery

"What systems do you need to reconcile between? How many transactions per month? Do you have API access?"

### Authority Discovery

"Who makes budget decisions for tools like this? What's your budget approval process? What's your timeline?"

### Budget Discovery

"What's your budget for reconciliation tools? How do you justify tool purchases? What would make this a no-brainer purchase?"

### Qualification Decision

**If Qualified (4+ points):**
"Based on what you've shared, Settler sounds like a great fit. Would you like to see a demo?"

**If Not Qualified (<4 points):**
"I appreciate your time. Based on what you've shared, Settler may not be the right fit right now. [Disqualify politely, reconnect later]"

## Disqualification Scripts

### Volume Too Low

"Thanks for your interest in Settler. Based on your volume (<1K transactions/month), Settler is designed for companies doing 10K+ transactions/month. For smaller volumes, manual reconciliation or spreadsheets are usually sufficient. I'd be happy to reconnect when you're processing more volume."

### Wrong Use Case

"Thanks for your interest in Settler. Based on your needs, Settler is designed for companies reconciling between multiple systems (e.g., Stripe + Shopify + QuickBooks). If you're only using one system, you might be better served by that system's native features. I'd be happy to reconnect when you're integrating multiple systems."

### No Budget Authority

"Thanks for your interest in Settler. Settler requires budget approval. I'd be happy to connect with your finance/operations team who has budget authority. Can you introduce me to the decision maker?"

### Custom Requirements

"Thanks for your interest in Settler. Settler is a SaaS platform with standard integrations. If you need extensive custom development or on-premise deployment, we're not the right fit. I'd be happy to recommend custom development partners or enterprise platforms that might be better suited."

## Qualification Tracking

### CRM Fields

- **Problem Clarity:** Yes/No
- **Data Readiness:** Yes/No
- **Authority:** Yes/No
- **Timeline:** Yes/No
- **Budget Signal:** Yes/No
- **Qualification Score:** 0-5
- **Qualification Level:** Highly Qualified / Qualified / Marginally Qualified / Disqualified

### Metrics

- **Qualification Rate:** % of leads that qualify (target: 30%+)
- **Conversion Rate:** % of qualified leads that convert (target: 30%+)
- **Time-to-Qualify:** Days from contact to qualification (target: <7 days)
