# Daily Operating Checklist

**Last Updated:** 2025-01-20  
**Status:** Revenue Bootstrap - Active  
**Purpose:** Daily checklist for founder operations

## Overview

This checklist helps you:

- **Stay focused:** Know what to do each day
- **Track progress:** Monitor daily metrics
- **Prevent issues:** Catch problems early
- **Maintain momentum:** Keep revenue engine running

**Philosophy:** Small daily actions compound into big results.

---

## Morning Routine (30 minutes)

### Check Metrics (10 minutes)

- [ ] **MRR:** Check current MRR, compare to target
- [ ] **New Customers:** Check new signups, conversions
- [ ] **Churn:** Check cancellations, at-risk customers
- [ ] **Pipeline:** Review pipeline status, next actions

### Review Alerts (10 minutes)

- [ ] **Support Tickets:** Review new support tickets, prioritize
- [ ] **System Alerts:** Check system health, errors
- [ ] **Customer Alerts:** Review customer health signals
- [ ] **Revenue Alerts:** Check billing issues, payment failures

### Plan Day (10 minutes)

- [ ] **Prioritize Tasks:** Identify top 3 priorities for today
- [ ] **Schedule Calls:** Schedule customer calls, demos
- [ ] **Block Time:** Block time for focused work
- [ ] **Set Goals:** Set daily goals, track progress

---

## Revenue Activities (2-3 hours)

### Outreach (1 hour)

- [ ] **Cold Emails:** Send 10 cold emails to prospects
- [ ] **Follow-Ups:** Follow up on previous emails (Day 5, Day 10)
- [ ] **LinkedIn:** Connect with prospects, engage with content
- [ ] **Referrals:** Ask for referrals from existing customers

### Demos (1-2 hours)

- [ ] **Demo Prep:** Prepare for demos (review use case, prepare demo)
- [ ] **Run Demos:** Conduct 2-3 demos
- [ ] **Follow-Up:** Send demo follow-up emails, pilot invites

### Pipeline Management (30 minutes)

- [ ] **Update CRM:** Update pipeline status, notes
- [ ] **Qualify Leads:** Review new leads, qualify/disqualify
- [ ] **Move Deals:** Move deals through pipeline stages
- [ ] **Forecast:** Update revenue forecast

---

## Customer Success Activities (1-2 hours)

### Pilot Management (1 hour)

- [ ] **Pilot Check-Ins:** Check in on active pilots (Day 3, Day 7, Day 14)
- [ ] **Pilot Support:** Help pilots with setup, issues
- [ ] **Conversion Discussions:** Discuss conversion with pilots ending soon

### Customer Support (30 minutes)

- [ ] **Support Tickets:** Respond to support tickets (24-48 hour SLA)
- [ ] **Customer Questions:** Answer customer questions
- [ ] **Issue Resolution:** Resolve customer issues

### Customer Health (30 minutes)

- [ ] **Health Checks:** Review customer health signals
- [ ] **Risk Signals:** Identify at-risk customers
- [ ] **Interventions:** Take preventive action for at-risk customers

---

## Product & Operations (1-2 hours)

### Product Review (30 minutes)

- [ ] **Usage Analytics:** Review usage analytics, identify trends
- [ ] **Feature Usage:** Review feature usage, identify opportunities
- [ ] **Bug Reports:** Review bug reports, prioritize fixes
- [ ] **Feature Requests:** Review feature requests, prioritize

### Operations Review (30 minutes)

- [ ] **System Health:** Check system health, performance
- [ ] **Error Logs:** Review error logs, identify issues
- [ ] **Cost Monitoring:** Review infrastructure costs, optimize
- [ ] **Security:** Review security alerts, address issues

---

## Evening Routine (30 minutes)

### Review Day (15 minutes)

- [ ] **Completed Tasks:** Review completed tasks, celebrate wins
- [ ] **Blockers:** Identify blockers, plan solutions
- [ ] **Learnings:** Document learnings, insights
- [ ] **Metrics:** Review daily metrics, compare to goals

### Plan Tomorrow (15 minutes)

- [ ] **Prioritize:** Identify top 3 priorities for tomorrow
- [ ] **Schedule:** Schedule calls, demos, focused work
- [ ] **Prepare:** Prepare for tomorrow's activities
- [ ] **Set Goals:** Set daily goals for tomorrow

---

## Weekly Focus Areas

### Monday: Outreach & Pipeline

**Focus:** New outreach, pipeline review, week planning

**Activities:**

- Send 20 cold emails
- Review pipeline, prioritize leads
- Plan week's activities
- Schedule demos for week

---

### Tuesday: Demos & Sales

**Focus:** Demos, sales conversations, conversions

**Activities:**

- Conduct 3-4 demos
- Follow up on demos
- Discuss conversions with pilots
- Close deals

---

### Wednesday: Customer Success

**Focus:** Customer support, pilot management, health checks

**Activities:**

- Check in on pilots
- Respond to support tickets
- Review customer health
- Take preventive actions

---

### Thursday: Product & Operations

**Focus:** Product improvements, operations, optimization

**Activities:**

- Review usage analytics
- Prioritize product improvements
- Optimize operations
- Review costs

---

### Friday: Review & Planning

**Focus:** Week review, metrics review, next week planning

**Activities:**

- Review week's metrics
- Review week's activities
- Plan next week
- Update pipeline

---

## Daily Metrics to Track

### Revenue Metrics

- MRR
- New customers
- Churn rate
- Conversion rate

### Pipeline Metrics

- Leads in pipeline
- Demos scheduled
- Pilots active
- Conversions pending

### Customer Metrics

- Support tickets
- Customer health scores
- At-risk customers
- Churn risk signals
