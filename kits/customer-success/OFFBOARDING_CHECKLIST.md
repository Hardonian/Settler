# Offboarding Checklist

**Last Updated:** 2025-01-20  
**Status:** Revenue Bootstrap - Active  
**Purpose:** Checklist for offboarding customers who don't convert or cancel

## Overview

This checklist helps you:
- **Handle cancellations:** Process cancellations gracefully
- **Export data:** Help customers export their data
- **Gather feedback:** Understand why they're leaving
- **Maintain relationship:** Keep door open for future

**Philosophy:** Offboard gracefully, learn from feedback, maintain relationship.

---

## Offboarding Triggers

### Pilot Non-Conversion
**Trigger:** Pilot ends, customer doesn't convert

**Action:** Offboard gracefully, gather feedback, reconnect later

---

### Cancellation Request
**Trigger:** Customer requests cancellation

**Action:** Process cancellation, export data, gather feedback

---

### Account Inactivity
**Trigger:** Account inactive for 90+ days

**Action:** Check in, offer help, or offboard if no response

---

## Offboarding Process

### Step 1: Acknowledge Request
**Action:** Acknowledge cancellation/non-conversion request

**Email Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I understand you're [cancelling / not converting]. I'm sorry to see you go, but I respect your decision.

Before you go, I'd like to:
1. Help you export your data
2. Understand why you're leaving (feedback)
3. Keep the door open for future

Let me know if you'd like to schedule a quick call or if you have questions.

[Your Name]
```

---

### Step 2: Export Data
**Action:** Help customer export their data

**Email Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

Here's how to export your data:

**Export Options:**
1. **Console Export:** Go to Settings → Export Data
2. **API Export:** Use our API to export data
3. **Manual Export:** I can help you export manually

**Data Retention:**
- Your data will be retained for 30 days after cancellation
- After 30 days, data will be permanently deleted
- Export your data before then

**Need help?** Reply to this email or contact support@settler.io

[Your Name]
```

---

### Step 3: Gather Feedback
**Action:** Understand why they're leaving

**Email Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I'd love to understand why you're [cancelling / not converting]. Your feedback helps us improve.

**Quick Survey:**
1. What was the main reason for [cancellation / non-conversion]?
2. What could we have done better?
3. Would you consider Settler in the future?

**Optional:** Schedule a 10-minute call to discuss: [calendar link]

[Your Name]
```

---

### Step 4: Process Cancellation
**Action:** Process cancellation, confirm timeline

**Email Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

Your cancellation has been processed. Here's what to expect:

**Timeline:**
- Access continues until [end of billing period]
- Data retained for 30 days after cancellation
- Data permanently deleted after 30 days

**Export Data:**
- Export your data before [date]
- Go to Settings → Export Data

**Reconnect:**
- If you change your mind, you can reactivate anytime
- I'll reconnect in 3 months to see if your situation has changed

**Questions?** Reply to this email or contact support@settler.io

[Your Name]
```

---

### Step 5: Follow-Up
**Action:** Follow up after cancellation, reconnect later

**Email Template (3 Months Later):**
```
Hi [Name],

I wanted to reconnect and see how things are going. Have your reconciliation needs changed?

If you're still dealing with reconciliation challenges, I'd love to discuss how Settler might help. We've made some improvements since you last used it.

**Want to reconnect?** Reply to this email or schedule a call: [calendar link]

[Your Name]
```

---

## Offboarding Checklist

### Pre-Offboarding
- [ ] **Acknowledge request:** Respond to cancellation/non-conversion request
- [ ] **Schedule call:** Offer to discuss (if appropriate)
- [ ] **Prepare data export:** Guide customer on data export

### During Offboarding
- [ ] **Export data:** Help customer export their data
- [ ] **Gather feedback:** Understand why they're leaving
- [ ] **Process cancellation:** Process cancellation, confirm timeline
- [ ] **Set expectations:** Explain data retention, access timeline

### Post-Offboarding
- [ ] **Send confirmation:** Send cancellation confirmation email
- [ ] **Document feedback:** Record feedback in CRM
- [ ] **Schedule reconnect:** Schedule reconnect in 3-6 months
- [ ] **Update pipeline:** Update pipeline status

## Feedback Categories

### Common Reasons for Non-Conversion

1. **Wrong Use Case**
   - "Not the right fit for our needs"
   - "Doesn't solve our problem"
   - "Wrong use case"

2. **Budget Constraints**
   - "No budget"
   - "Too expensive"
   - "Can't justify cost"

3. **Technical Issues**
   - "Too complex"
   - "Integration issues"
   - "Technical difficulties"

4. **Timing**
   - "Not the right time"
   - "Other priorities"
   - "Need to wait"

5. **Competition**
   - "Found another solution"
   - "Using competitor"
   - "Better alternative"

---

## Feedback Analysis

### Track Feedback
- **Category:** Wrong use case, budget, technical, timing, competition
- **Details:** Specific feedback, concerns, suggestions
- **Action Items:** What to improve based on feedback

### Use Feedback
- **Product improvements:** Address common issues
- **Sales improvements:** Improve qualification, messaging
- **Support improvements:** Better onboarding, documentation

---

## Offboarding Best Practices

### Do's
- ✅ **Be respectful:** Respect their decision, don't pressure
- ✅ **Help export data:** Make data export easy
- ✅ **Gather feedback:** Understand why they're leaving
- ✅ **Maintain relationship:** Keep door open for future
- ✅ **Learn from feedback:** Use feedback to improve

### Don'ts
- ❌ **Don't pressure:** Don't try to convince them to stay
- ❌ **Don't ignore feedback:** Listen to concerns, learn from them
- ❌ **Don't burn bridges:** Maintain professional relationship
- ❌ **Don't forget follow-up:** Reconnect later, don't disappear
