# Metrics Definitions and Board Pack

## Canonical definitions

- ARR = 12 x month-end committed recurring revenue (exclude one-time implementation).
- MRR = normalized monthly recurring subscription + committed recurring support.
- GRR = (Start MRR - contraction - churn) / Start MRR.
- NRR = (Start MRR - churn - contraction + expansion) / Start MRR.
- Logo churn = lost customers / starting customers.
- CAC payback = CAC / monthly gross profit from new cohort.
- Gross margin = (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue.
- Implementation margin = (implementation revenue - implementation delivery cost)/implementation revenue.

## What does not count

- Pipeline, unsigned LOIs, and unpaid pilots do not count as ARR/MRR.
- One-time professional services do not count as ARR.

## Board pack sections

1. Revenue and retention
2. Pipeline and conversion quality
3. Implementation throughput and risks
4. Support health and renewals
5. Cash/collections and runway view
6. Top risks + required decisions

## Narrative template

- What changed?
- Why it changed?
- What management did?
- What decisions are requested?
