# Cash Collections and Dunning Policy

## Default terms

- Standard: Net-30
- Enterprise exception: Net-45 with approval

## Reminder cadence

- Day -5: upcoming reminder
- Day +3: gentle overdue
- Day +10: collections notice
- Day +20: escalation to founder
- Day +30: service restriction review (per contract)

## Escalation thresholds

- > $10k overdue or >30 days: founder intervention required
- repeated late payer: prepay or tighter terms at renewal

## Write-off and courtesy

- Write-offs require founder + finance signoff.
- Courtesy remains professional; no punitive language in customer comms.

## Service restriction boundaries

Never silently cut production-critical access without documented notice path and contractual basis.
