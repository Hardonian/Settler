# Founder Dashboard Spec

## Weekly minimum metrics

- New pipeline, pilot starts, pilot conversions
- Bookings and weighted ARR
- Implementation backlog age
- Open high-severity support tickets
- Overdue AR and oldest invoice age

## Monthly minimum metrics

- MRR/ARR, GRR/NRR, logo churn
- Gross margin and implementation margin
- CAC payback trend
- Forecast variance vs plan

## Intervention alerts

- Discount rate > policy threshold
- Implementation queue > capacity for 2+ weeks
- Collections >15% of AR overdue >30 days
- Renewal risk on top 20% ARR accounts
