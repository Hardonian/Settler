## Purpose

> This is practical internal guidance, not authoritative accounting advice. Confirm final treatment with CPA.

## Revenue Categories

| Stream             | Treatment                                      |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Subscriptions**  | Recognize ratably over service period (1-mo)   |
| **Annual Prepay**  | Recognized ratably (1/12) per month            |
| **Implementation** | Recognize as performance obligations delivered |
| **Usage Overages** | Recognize in period earned (measurable)        |
| **Services (T&M)** | Recognize as delivered/accepted                |

---

## Billing vs Recognition

1. **Annual upfront invoices** create deferred revenue until earned monthly.
2. **Cash receipt timing** does not equal revenue recognition timing.
3. **Usage overages** are recognized when reported, even if billed in arrears.

---

## Credits, Refunds, and Service Failures

- **Approved credits** reduce recognized revenue or deferred revenue depending on timing.
- **Material service failures** may require reversal entries.

---

## Pilot Treatment

- **Free pilots:** no revenue.
- **Paid pilots:** recognize over pilot period per obligation completion.

---

## CPA Boundary List

- Multi-element enterprise arrangements
- Variable consideration constraints
- Tax nexus and indirect taxes
- Any non-standard cancellation/refund clause
