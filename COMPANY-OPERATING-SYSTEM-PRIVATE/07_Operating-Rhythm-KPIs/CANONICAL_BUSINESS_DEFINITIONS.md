# Canonical Business Definitions

- **Pilot:** timeboxed validation engagement with explicit success criteria.
- **Implementation:** scoped onboarding delivery work, distinct from support.
- **Support:** break/fix and product usage guidance within plan boundaries.
- **Expansion:** additional scope under existing customer relationship.
- **Renewal-ready:** account has value proof, sponsor alignment, and no unresolved critical blockers.
- **Commercial exception:** approved deviation from standard pricing/terms documented in register.
