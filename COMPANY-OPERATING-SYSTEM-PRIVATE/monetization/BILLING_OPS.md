## Overview

## Stripe Setup

### Required Stripe Configuration

1. **Products and Prices**
   - Free tier: No Stripe product
   - Starter: $29/month, $290/year
   - Growth: $99/month, $990/year
   - Scale: $299/month, $2,990/year
   - Enterprise: Custom (manual invoice)

2. **Payment Methods**
   - Credit cards (default)
   - ACH bank transfers ($500+ customers)

3. **Webhooks Required**
   - `customer.subscription.created`
   - `customer.subscription.updated`
   - `customer.subscription.deleted`
   - `invoice.payment_succeeded`
   - `invoice.payment_failed`
   - `invoice.paid`

### Stripe Dashboard Access

- Founder has full access
- Operational access for billing queries
- No shared credentials

## Subscription Lifecycle

### New Subscription Flow

1. Customer selects plan on website
2. Redirect to Stripe Checkout
3. Customer enters payment info
4. Stripe creates subscription
5. Webhook triggers account provisioning
6. Welcome email sent

### Upgrade Flow

1. Customer selects new plan
2. Stripe prorates difference
3. Immediate upgrade
4. Confirmation email sent

### Downgrade Flow

1. Customer selects lower plan
2. Change takes effect at billing cycle end
3. No refund for current period
4. Warning email sent before downgrade

### Cancellation Flow

1. Customer initiates cancellation
2. Access continues until period end
3. No refund (per terms)
4. Final confirmation email
5. Customer data retained 30 days

## Payment Handling

### Successful Payments

- Automatic invoice generation
- Payment receipt to customer email
- Dashboard update
- Revenue tracking

### Failed Payments

- Retry schedule: Day 1, Day 3, Day 7
- Customer email after each failure
- Account suspension after 3 failures
- 7-day grace period
- Data deletion after grace period (if no conversion)

### Payment Retry Logic

```
Day 0: Initial charge
Day 1: First retry
Day 3: Second retry
Day 7: Third retry + final notice
Day 14: Account suspended
Day 30: Data deletion (non-converting pilots)
```

## Invoice Management

### Automatic Invoices

- Generated by Stripe
- Sent to customer email
- Available in Stripe Dashboard
- PDF download available

### Manual Invoices (Enterprise)

- Created in Stripe Dashboard
- Custom line items
- Net-30 or Net-60 terms
- Follow-up reminders automated

### Invoice Components

- Company name and address
- Customer name and address
- Line items with descriptions
- Subtotal
- Discounts (if applicable)
- Tax (if applicable)
- Total
- Payment terms
- Due date

## Overage Billing

### Calculation

- Tracked by Settler platform
- Synced to Stripe daily
- Invoiced at end of billing period

### Overage Charge

- $0.01 per reconciliation over limit
- Appears as line item on invoice
- Clear breakdown in invoice description

### Overage Prevention

- Dashboard warning at 80% usage
- Email notification at 90% usage
- Email notification at 100% + overage begins

## Refund Policy

### Standard Refunds

- No refunds for partial subscription periods
- Exception: Service failure (case-by-case)

### Enterprise Refunds

- Per contract terms
- Generally no refunds
- Credit toward future services

### Refund Process

1. Customer requests refund
2. Verify service failure or exception criteria
3. Process refund in Stripe (if approved)
4. Document in customer record
5. Log decision in `decisions/`

## Common Issues

### Customer Can't Update Payment

1. Verify card expiration
2. Check billing address
3. Try different card
4. Contact Stripe support if needed

### Customer Overcharged

1. Pull Stripe invoice
2. Verify calculation
3. If error: Issue refund
4. Document and log decision

### Subscription Not Activating

1. Check Stripe subscription status
2. Verify webhook delivery
3. Check Settler account provisioning
4. Manually trigger if needed
