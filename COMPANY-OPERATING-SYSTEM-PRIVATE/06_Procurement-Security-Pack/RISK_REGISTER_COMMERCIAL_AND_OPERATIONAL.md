# Commercial and Operational Risk Register

| Risk                     | Impact                    | Mitigation                                     | Owner      |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Revenue concentration    | renewal shock             | diversify pipeline and enforce renewal cadence | Founder    |
| Founder dependency       | execution bottleneck      | RACI + template-based operations               | Founder    |
| Implementation overload  | delayed value realization | capacity gating + paid implementation          | Impl owner |
| Support sprawl           | margin erosion            | support boundaries by plan                     | CS owner   |
| Tax/compliance ambiguity | liability exposure        | quarterly CPA/legal review cycle               | Finance    |
| Legal overcommitment     | delivery breach risk      | redline playbook + walk-away rules             | Legal      |
