# Initial Operating Decisions

## Decisions made in this pass

1. Canonical internal packaging uses Starter/Growth/Enterprise with controlled implementation and usage components.
2. All non-standard pricing/legal/support concessions require explicit exception record and expiry.
3. Pilot motion is timeboxed and conversion-evidence driven.
4. Monthly close is mandatory and evidence-backed before executive reporting.

## Judgment calls made

- Chose a conservative contracting posture to avoid overpromising beyond current capability claims in legacy docs.
- Standardized support promises to avoid conflicting historical response commitments.

## Revisit triggers

- After first 3 paying customers
- After first enterprise procurement cycle
- After first annual financial close
