# MSA Red Flag Clauses

## High-risk clauses to flag immediately

- Unlimited indemnity or uncapped liability
- Customer-defined SLA penalties with no cap
- Broad security warranty guaranteeing zero incidents
- Data residency promises unsupported by current architecture
- Refund rights disconnected from service period

## Operational danger signals

- Contract terms requiring manual bespoke behavior for one tenant
- Audit obligations that exceed team capacity
- Custom breach-notification clocks that conflict with incident process

## Non-negotiable truth boundary

If product capability cannot support it today, do not promise it in contract text.
