# Redlines and Negotiation Playbook

## Common asks and posture

| Ask               | Acceptable                     | Caution                     | Walk-away                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Uptime SLA        | standard tier SLA              | custom credits cap          | unlimited penalties                   |
| Security warranty | reasonable efforts + controls  | broad strict liability      | absolute "breach impossible" language |
| Data residency    | current supported regions only | roadmap with no commitment  | hard promise without capability       |
| Liability cap     | fees paid in prior 12 months   | 24-month cap for enterprise | uncapped general liability            |
| Payment terms     | net-30/45                      | net-60 with controls        | net-90 + no enforcement               |

## Negotiation sequence

1. Align on business outcome and scope.
2. Lock commercial terms.
3. Redline legal terms against playbook.
4. Escalate exceptions and log decision.

## Product-truth constraints

Never contractually promise unsupported auth, tenancy, backup, or compliance states.
