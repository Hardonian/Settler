## Key Terms for Operators

### Subscription Tiers

| Tier       | Price                | Limit          |
| ---------- | -------------------- | -------------- |
| Starter    | $99/mo or $990/yr    | 1,000 jobs/mo  |
| Pro        | $299/mo or $2,990/yr | 10,000 jobs/mo |
| Enterprise | Custom               | Unlimited      |

### Overage

- Automatic upgrade to next tier, OR
- $0.01 per job over limit
- No refunds for overages

### Payment

- Due in advance
- Failed payments → service suspension
- No refunds for partial periods

### SLA

| Tier       | Uptime |
| ---------- | ------ |
| Starter    | 99.5%  |
| Pro        | 99.9%  |
| Enterprise | 99.99% |

- Provide services as described

### Don't Have To

- Guarantee uninterrupted service
- Refund for partial periods
- Provide unlimited usage (Enterprise has fair use)

## Common Customer Questions

### "Can I get a refund?"

> No refunds for partial periods. Refunds at our discretion for service failures.

### "What happens if I exceed my limit?"

> We charge $0.01 per job over limit, or auto-upgrade to next tier.

### "Can I cancel anytime?"

> Yes, effective at end of billing period.

### "Who owns my data?"

> You own your data. We can only use it to provide services.

### "What if Settler is acquired?"

> Your data remains yours. New owner must honor existing terms.
