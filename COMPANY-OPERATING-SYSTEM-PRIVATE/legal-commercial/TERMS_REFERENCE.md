## Pilot Agreement Summary

### Pilot Scope

- **Included:** API access, Developer Console, standard integrations, email support
- **Excluded:** Custom integrations, on-premise, white-label, dedicated support, SLAs

### Pilot Period

- **Duration:** 14 days (extendable to 30)
- **Termination:** Either party, any time, written notice

### Conversion Criteria

1. Onboarding complete within 7 days
2. At least one successful reconciliation
3. Processing at least 1K transactions
4. 95%+ match rate achieved
5. Logged in at least 3 times

### Data Handling

- Customer retains data ownership
- Data deleted upon pilot end (unless converted)
- 7-day grace period after pilot

## Common Operator Questions

**Q: A customer wants custom SLA terms**  
A: Refer to MSA template. Founder approval needed for non-standard terms. Legal review for liability changes.

**Q: A customer wants unlimited liability**  
A: Non-negotiable. Standard cap is 12 months fees. Escalate to legal.

**Q: A customer wants data residency in specific region**  
A: Enterprise only. Assess technical feasibility. Custom contract needed.

**Q: A customer wants us to be processor vs controller**  
A: Per GDPR, we're typically processor. DPA required. Legal review for specific arrangements.

**Q: Customer claims our ToS violates their rights**  
A: Escalate immediately to legal. Do not make commitments.
