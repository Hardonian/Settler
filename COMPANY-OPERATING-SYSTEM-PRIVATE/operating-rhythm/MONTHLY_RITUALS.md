## Overview

This document defines the monthly operating cadence for Settler, including the monthly financial close.

---

## Monthly Rhythm

### Day 1-5: Monthly Close

See `../finance/MONTHLY_CLOSE.md` for financial close procedures.

**Summary:**

- Day 1: Close begins, export data
- Day 2: Revenue reconciliation, MRR calculation
- Day 3: Cost reconciliation, COGS calculation
- Day 4: Margin analysis, variance review
- Day 5: Financial summary complete

---

### Day 5-7: Monthly Review

**Activities:**

1. **Review financial close**
   - MRR, ARR, revenue by tier
   - Gross margin
   - Burn rate
   - Runway

2. **Review operating metrics**
   - Customer acquisition
   - Customer retention
   - Product usage
   - Support volume

3. **Assess progress vs plan**
   - On track with goals?
   - Need to adjust?
   - Decisions needed?

## Monthly Decision Review

### First Week of Month

Review decisions from previous month:

**Questions:**

- Were the decisions correct?
- Do we need to revisit?
- Any cascading decisions needed?
- Should any become policies?

**Decision Review Template:**

```markdown
# Decision Review: [Title]

**Original Date:** [Date]
**Review Date:** [Date]

## Original Decision

[What we decided]

## What Happened

[Outcomes since]

## Assessment

[Was it right?]

## Follow-up Actions

[Any changes needed]
```

---

## Monthly Prioritization

### Begin Next Month Planning

1. **Review last month's performance**
   - What worked?
   - What didn't?
   - What surprised us?

2. **Set next month's priorities**
   - Top 3-5 focus areas
   - Success metrics
   - Resource allocation

3. **Identify decisions needed**
   - What requires decision?
   - Who needs to decide?
   - What's the deadline?
