# Customer Health and Renewal Model

## Health inputs

- Usage trend vs contracted scope
- Exception workflow adoption depth
- Proof-of-value artifacts produced
- Support burden and unresolved high-severity tickets

## Renewal risk indicators

- Declining operator usage
- Repeat unresolved critical incidents
- Chronic billing disputes
- Missing executive sponsor engagement

## Expansion signals

- Additional teams/workflows requesting adoption
- Stable first workflow + measurable value gains
- Procurement trust already established

## Save motion

Trigger save plan 120 days pre-renewal for at-risk accounts.
