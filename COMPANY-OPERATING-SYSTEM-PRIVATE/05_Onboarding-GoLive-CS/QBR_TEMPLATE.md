# QBR Template

## 1. Value/proof summary

- Outcomes achieved this quarter
- Evidence artifacts reviewed

## 2. Adoption summary

- Active operators and workflow frequency
- Exception handling quality trend

## 3. Issues and risks

- Open risks, owners, target resolution dates

## 4. Forward plan

- Next quarter focus
- Truthful roadmap framing (committed vs exploratory)

## 5. Renewal/expansion discussion

- Renewal readiness score
- Expansion hypotheses and required proof
