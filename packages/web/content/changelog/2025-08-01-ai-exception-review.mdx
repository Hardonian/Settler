## What Changed

The **exception review UI** now includes AI-generated context for every mismatch. The AI compresses triage time by surfacing relevant context, suggesting classification labels, and flagging anomalies — without making autonomous decisions.

### How It Works

When a reviewer opens a mismatch, the AI layer:

1. **Summarizes the mismatch** — explains the variance in plain language (amount difference, timing offset, field-level breakdown)
2. **Suggests a classification** — offers a likely category (timing difference, duplicate, data-entry error, genuine variance) with a confidence score
3. **Flags anomalies** — highlights if this mismatch pattern is unusual relative to recent runs or historical norms
4. **Shows similar past exceptions** — surfaces resolved mismatches with similar characteristics for reference

### What the AI Does NOT Do

- The AI does not resolve mismatches. Every exception requires an explicit human decision.
- The AI suggestion is advisory. Reviewers can accept, modify, or ignore the suggestion.
- All human decisions are captured in the audit trail with the reviewer's documented reason.
- The AI layer is stateless per run — no training on your financial data.

### Audit Trail

The audit trail now records:
- Whether the AI suggestion was shown to the reviewer
- Whether the reviewer accepted or overrode the suggestion
- The reviewer's documented resolution reason (required regardless of AI involvement)

This ensures the review record is complete and defensible for compliance purposes.

## Configuration

AI-assisted review is enabled by default on all plans. It can be disabled per-workspace in Settings → Review → AI Assistance.
