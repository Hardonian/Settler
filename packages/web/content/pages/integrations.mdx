## Provider-agnostic by default

Settler treats integrations as adapters. You can use built-in connectors or bring your own data pipelines and map into the canonical schema.

## Common sources

- Payment processors (e.g., Stripe, Adyen, PayPal)
- Commerce platforms (e.g., Shopify, WooCommerce)
- Accounting exports (e.g., QuickBooks, Xero)
- Internal ledgers and data warehouses

## Build your own adapter

Adapters are small, explicit mappings. If you have a custom source, you can normalize your data directly without waiting on a vendor-specific integration.
