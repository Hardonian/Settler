## Why Settler exists

Financial reconciliation is high-stakes and often opaque. Settler focuses on deterministic, inspectable workflows that surface variances instead of hiding them.

## Principles

- **Deterministic outputs** over black-box heuristics
- **Human-in-the-loop** review for judgment calls
- **Provider-agnostic** data models
- **Open-source first** development

## Who it’s for

Teams that want reliable, auditable reconciliation infrastructure without turning judgment into automation.
