# Settler: Product Requirements Document (PRD) ## 1. Executive Summary Settler is an enterprise-grade financial reconciliation and operational control platform. It is designed to provide finance and operations teams with a trustworthy, auditable, and high-density workspace for managing complex transaction flows, exceptions, and compliance requirements. ## 2. Information Architecture & Route Map ### 2.1 Core Operations - `/workspace` - Reconciliation Queue (Default) - `/workspace/investigate/:id` - Investigation Detail (Overlay/Full-screen) - `/exceptions` - Exceptions Dashboard - `/exceptions/:id` - Exception Investigation Detail - `/runs` - Processing Monitor List - `/runs/:id` - Run Granular Logs & Detail ### 2.2 Configuration - `/matching-rules` - Rules Management - `/matching-rules/editor/:id` - Logic Builder & Simulation - `/connectors` - Integration Gallery - `/connectors/:id` - Connector Health & Sync Logs - `/connectors/setup` - Setup Wizard (Multi-step) ### 2.3 Financial Control - `/period-close` - Period Close Readiness & Checklist - `/settlement` - Payout & Settlement Queue - `/reports` - Report Builder & Catalog ### 2.4 Administration - `/admin/team` - Member Management - `/admin/roles` - RBAC Matrix - `/admin/audit-log` - Global Activity Explorer - `/admin/security` - Session & API Key Governance --- ## 3. Core System Principles ### 3.1 Operational Truth (Safety First) - **Determinism**: Every system decision must be backed by a specific rule and audit-grade evidence. - **No Silent Assumptions**: Ambiguous matches or incomplete data must be explicitly flagged to the operator. - **Auditability**: Every state change is attributed to an actor (user or system) with a timestamp and "before/after" context. ### 3.2 Temporal Truth - **Dual Timeframes**: Distinguish between "Event Time" (when the transaction occurred) and "Processing Time" (when Settler ingested/acted on it). - **Aging & SLAs**: Critical items are tracked by age buckets (0-24h, 24-48h, etc.) and SLA deadlines. ### 3.3 Risk & Priority - **Risk Scoring**: Items are weighted by amount, age, and systemic confidence. - **Visual Hierarchy**: High-risk entities use semantic color (Red/Amber) and elevated positioning in queues. --- ## 4. UI/UX Standards ### 4.1 Design System - **Tone**: Calm, professional, and dense. - **Palette**: Neutral Slate base with semantic accents (Emerald for Success, Amber for Warning, Rose for Critical). - **Typography**: Optimized for data legibility (tabular numbers, clear hierarchy). - **Layout**: Stable, grid-aligned, avoiding jitter and layout shifts. ### 4.2 Progressive Disclosure - Summary views for rapid scanning. - Deep-dive drawers or full-screen sheets for forensic investigation. - Hidden technical metadata (JSON, raw logs) available via toggle. ### 4.3 Navigation & Continuity - **Non-destructive**: Filters and selections must persist during drill-downs. - **Overlay Priority**: Prefer drawers/modals for detail views to maintain background context. --- ## 5. Failure & Degraded State Handling - **Banners & Watermarks**: Explicitly signal when data is stale or a connector is lagging. - **Disabled Actions**: Block high-risk actions (like Period Close) if system invariants are violated or data is incomplete. - **Recovery Paths**: Provide direct links to retry failed runs or re-auth broken connectors. --- ## 6. Audit & Evidence Pack - **Evidence Packs**: Tamper-proof ZIP exports containing scoped transactions, logs, and attestations. - **SHA-256 Verification**: Every export includes a unique hash for external verification. --- ## 7.
