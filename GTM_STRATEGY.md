#### 1.5 Free Tier Strategy

**Timeline:** Months 1-6

**Free Tier Limits:**
- 1,000 reconciliations/month
- 100 receipt parses/month
- 100k feature flag evaluations/month
- 2 platform adapters
- 7-day log retention

**Purpose:**
- **Acquisition:** Lower barrier to entry
- **Product-Led Growth:** Self-service onboarding

**Conversion Strategy:**
- **Usage-Based Upsells:** "You've used 80% of your free tier"
- **Feature Gating:** Advanced features require paid plan
- **Support:** Free tier = community support, paid = email support

**Success Metrics:**
- 1,000 free tier users
- 10% conversion rate (100 paying customers)

## Risk Mitigation

### Market Risks

**Risk:** Market doesn't need dedicated reconciliation service  
**Mitigation:** Validate with beta users, iterate based on feedback, expand to adjacent markets

**Risk:** Market saturation  
**Mitigation:** Build strong brand, focus on developer experience, maintain innovation speed

### Execution Risks

**Risk:** Customer acquisition cost higher than projected  
**Mitigation:** Focus on product-led growth, content marketing, optimize conversion funnel

**Risk:** Churn rate higher than 5%  
**Mitigation:** Strong onboarding, comprehensive documentation, proactive support, product improvements

### Competitive Risks

**Risk:** Large competitor (Stripe, Intuit) builds competing product  
**Mitigation:** Build strong moats (developer experience, network effects), focus on speed, partnerships

**Risk:** Price war  
**Mitigation:** Focus on value, not just price, build strong brand, differentiate on developer experience
