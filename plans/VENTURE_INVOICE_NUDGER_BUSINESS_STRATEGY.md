# Settler Business Strategy — Venture Invoice Nudger

## Strategic Fit

This feature compounds Settler’s core reconciliation moat by extending from passive reconciliation into active cashflow operations.

- Existing strength: transaction truth + reconciliation context
- New value: collections actioning with guardrails
- Outcome: direct, measurable revenue/cash impact for customers

## Revenue Thesis

### Why customers pay

- Collections mistakes are expensive (brand damage + wasted time)
- Speed of follow-up directly affects cash conversion
- Finance teams need explainability for every outreach decision

### Monetization path

1. Include dry-run insights in higher-tier plans to drive expansion
2. Charge for execute-mode volume and advanced policy automation
3. Add premium modules:
   - multichannel reminders
   - per-segment sequencing
   - approval workflows and compliance controls

## Competitive Edge

Most nudgers are rules-only and context-blind.

Settler differentiates with:
- Reconciliation-aware suppression
- Tenant-safe audit evidence for every action
- Unified transaction + invoice + recon model already in platform

## Execution Roadmap

### Phase 1 (now)
- Decision engine + persistence + run analytics
- Dry-run + execute-mode action tagging

### Phase 2
- Delivery integrations (email/SMS/CRM tasks)
- Human approval gating and escalation chains
- Retry and cadence policies

### Phase 3
- Outcome feedback loop
- Segment-level policy optimization
- ML-assisted prediction of best contact time + channel

## Operational Model

- Product owner: Settler Core Ops
- Growth owner: PMM + outbound founder-led sales
- Success owner: onboarding + weekly recovery review

## Risks & Mitigations

1. **False positives** → conservative suppression and human-in-loop rollout
2. **Over-automation risk** → strict approval controls + audit logs
3. **Data quality drift** → connector health checks + confidence thresholds

## 90-Day Targets

- 5 pilot tenants live
- measurable cash recovery uplift in at least 3 pilots
- false nudge rate below agreed threshold
- first paid expansion tied directly to nudger outcomes
